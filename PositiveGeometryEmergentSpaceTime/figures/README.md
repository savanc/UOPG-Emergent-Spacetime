# Figures used in the repository front page

Each figure is reproduced from one of the papers listed in the repository README. The scripts that generate them live with the paper sources, which are archived separately from this repository, so they are not included here; the numbers each figure shows are the ones tabulated in the repository README and in the programme README.

| File | What it shows | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| `mission-and-meters.png` | The target quantities (mass ratios, `N_c`, `N_gen`, chirality) and the two progress meters: 1 of 87 derivation-ledger items, 0 of 73 quantum numbers. | Paper II, figure illustrating the mission and the locked meters (R4-DM v3). |
| `result-verdicts.png` | Verdict mix of the 114 result reviews: 39 PASS, 18 MIXED, 16 ILL-DEFINED, 14 PARTIAL or CONDITIONAL, 13 FAIL or REFUTED, 10 NO-GO, 2 NOT-EVALUATED, 1 NUMEROLOGY, 1 EMPTY. | Paper II, from the record of the result reviews. |
| `blocked-routes.png` | How the 77 tagged closed routes are tagged: 35 NO-GO, 24 frontier theory, 21 contaminated, and the rest. | Paper II, from the blocked-route registry. |
| `associahedron-curvature.png` | Edge-mean Ollivier curvature of the associahedron flip graph against `n`, with the decagon at exactly zero, and the edge mix by quadrilateral type. | Curvature paper, exact computation over all edges. |

The images are unaltered renderings of the paper figures; no data was changed for this page.
