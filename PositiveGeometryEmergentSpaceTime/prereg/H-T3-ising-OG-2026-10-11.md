## Definitions fixed in advance

- **Ising network and correlations.**
  - Couplings J_e > 0 with x_e = tanh J_e.
  - Exact correlations by the high-temperature expansion:
    ⟨σ_iσ_j⟩ = Σ_{F : ∂F = {i,j}} x^F / Σ_{F : ∂F = ∅} x^F, with F ranging over edge subsets.
  - Exact rationals throughout.
- **GP map.** M ↦ M̃ (GP eq. 2.4). OG(n, 2n) := {Δ_I = Δ_{[2n]∖I}}. Positivity means all n×n minors ≥ 0.
- **The carrier (fixed now, before (c)):** ω_I := Π_e dlog sinh(2J_e).
  - Reason: Kramers–Wannier duality is the cyclic symmetry of OG≥0 (GP Thm 3.4) and acts as sinh 2J ↦ 1/sinh 2J.
  - This is the unique variable among {J, tanh J, e^{−2J}, sinh 2J} in which the dlog measure is KW-covariant (up to
    sign).
  - It is chosen for that reason, not by matching ABJM.
- **The ABJM measure.** Ω_A := the pull-back through φ of Lee's measure
  d^{k×2k}C δ^{k(k+1)/2}(C η Cᵀ) / (vol GL(k) · M_j M_{j+1} ⋯ M_{j+k−1}) (HW eq. 2.8).
  - η = diag(+, −, …), GP's real form (Prop. 5.1).
  - M_l are consecutive minors; both j = 1 and j = 2 are computed.
  - The δ-function measure is evaluated by the coarea formula in the gauge C = (I_k | c).
- **Ratio.** R := Ω_A / ω_I, as a function of the couplings.

## Targets and criteria

**(a) Cells.**
- Families: all edge subsets of
  - n = 3: triangle + centre with 3 spokes (64 subgraphs);
  - n = 4: 4-cycle + centre with 4 spokes (256 subgraphs).
  Random positive rational x_e. n = 6 is not attempted (honest scope).
- Checks:
  - (1) Δ_I = Δ_{I^c} exactly for all I.
  - (2) All minors ≥ 0.
  - (3) The Grassmann necklace gives a decorated permutation that is a fixed-point-free involution τ_I.
  - (4) The Jacobian rank of the correlations w.r.t. x equals xing(τ_I).
  - (5) τ_I equals the Lam (electrical) medial pairing τ_L of the *same graph* from H-T2.
- **PASS:** all checks hold for every subgraph. **FAIL:** any exact mismatch.

**(b) Lean.**
- For the n = 2 one-edge network, prove:
  - the six minors, the OG relation, positivity for 0 < σ < 1;
  - the top cell with τ = (13)(24) (by `decide`);
  - the identities σ = tanh J ⇒ (1 − σ²)/(2σ) = 1/sinh 2J, and s² + c² = 1, c/s = sinh 2J for s = sech 2J,
    c = tanh 2J.
- Optional: an n = 3 or 4 instance.
- **PASS:** the n = 2 items are proved.

**(c) Carrier + modulation (the falsifiable test).**
- k = 2: is R constant (in J)?
- k = 3: Y network (couplings L₁, L₂, L₃) and Δ network (K₁₂, K₁₃, K₂₃). Is R constant? If not, does R equal
  (up to a constant) an HWX-type loop factor? Candidates fixed now: 1 ± Π c_v, 1 ± Π s_v,
  (1 ± Π c_v)/(1 ± Π s_v).
- Star–triangle: is the log-Jacobian of (sinh 2L_i) ↦ (sinh 2K_ij) equal to ±1?
- Verdicts:
  - **PASS** ("carrier = amplitude measure"): R is constant at k = 2 and k = 3. Then the push-forward through the
    ABJM δ-functions reproduces the 4- and 6-pt leading singularities identically, because the integrand is Lee's.
  - **PARTIAL** ("modulated carrier"): R is constant at k = 2, but at k = 3 R is non-constant and equals an
    HWX-type J. The amplitude measure is then carrier × J, an extra network-intrinsic factor.
  - **FAIL:** R is non-constant at k = 2, or non-constant at k = 3 and not of HWX type.
  - **INCONCLUSIVE:** the branch or sign ambiguity can't be resolved. Both OG components and both j are checked.
- Normalisation caveat, stated up front: reproducing the 4-pt superamplitude δ³(P)δ⁶(Q)/(⟨12⟩⟨23⟩) *as a
  function* follows from integrand equality plus HW's evaluation (CITED). We will not independently redo the
  fermionic δ-function normalisation.
- The square roots in the 6-pt localisation are not evaluated; only the measure is compared.

**(d) Side test: E₄ vs the m = 2 amplituhedron A_{8,3,2}** (dimension match 6 = 3·2, from the carrier note).
- Setup:
  - Lam point C ∈ Gr≥0(3, 8) of the H-T2 top network (6 edges).
  - Z the 8×5 moment-curve matrix (t_i = i), positive.
  - Y = C Z.
- Checks:
  - (1) The image lies in A (⟨Y i i+1⟩ signs per AHTT, k = 3 sign flips of ⟨Y 1 i⟩).
  - (2) Full-dimensional: the Jacobian of (gauge-fixed Y) w.r.t. conductances has rank 6.
  - (3) For each facet of E₄ (c = 5 limits of single conductances → 0 or ∞), whether some ⟨Y i i+1⟩ vanishes on
    its image (a facet on ∂A) or none (an internal wall).
- **PASS:** full-dimensional, and ≥ 1 E₄ facet lands inside an amplituhedron facet (the image is a tile sharing
  boundary with A). **FAIL:** not full-dimensional, or no facet on ∂A. **INCONCLUSIVE:** limits ambiguous.
- Known before (carrier note): at n = 2 the image is a proper sub-segment whose endpoints are not on ∂A_{4,1,1}.

## Amendment A1 (2026-10-11, pushed before any (c) computation)

**What I noticed while preparing (c), from HW §2 alone, with nothing computed:**
- Lee's measure d^{k×2k}C δ(CηCᵀ)/(vol GL(k) · ΠM) has GL(k) weight −1. The weight is compensated only by the
  kinematic δ^{2|3}(C·Λ) (weight +1).
- So "Ω_A" as a stand-alone form depends on how the kinematic δ is normalised, i.e. on the gauge in which C is
  evaluated. HW themselves get dθ/s in the canonical gauge (columns 1, 2 = I, eq. 2.25) and dθ/(sc) in the cyclic
  gauge (columns 1, 3 = I, eq. 2.28).

**Amendment.**
- R is computed in **both** conventions:
  - (G1) first k columns = I, as originally pre-registered;
  - (G2) odd columns 1, 3, …, 2k−1 = I (HW's cyclic gauge, generalised uniformly in k).
- Each with j = 1 and j = 2.
- Verdict rule: **PASS/PARTIAL may be claimed only for a convention fixed uniformly in k** (G1 or G2, same choice at
  k = 2 and k = 3). I report which, and that the convention is an input.
- If R is constant in neither convention at k = 2, it is FAIL as before.
