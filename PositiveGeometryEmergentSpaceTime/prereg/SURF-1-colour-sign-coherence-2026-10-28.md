**Model.** Field φ = φ^a T^a (N×N, adjoint). Vertex = Tr(φφφ) summed over BOTH cyclic orientations, weight 1 each (= ½ d^{abc} in components; d-coupling, the colour structure of a real-coupled Tr φ³ with φ Hermitian). Propagator ⟨φ_ij φ_kl⟩ = δ_il δ_jk − (c/N) δ_ij δ_kl (= 2Σ_a T^a_{ij}T^a_{kl} at c = 1; c = 1 SU(N), c = 0 U(N)). Each closed index loop gives N. **Observable.** K_{G,ch}(N) = W(tree_ch ∘ G)/W(tree_ch ∘ tree_ch) (n = 2: W(theta)/W(single propagator loop)). Kinematic sign (derived on paper, stated as an assumption): in Euclidean kinematics with real coupling g, every one-loop scalar integral divided by its tree propagators is +g²·(positive), so sign(K) is the sign of the colour-weighted one-loop/tree interference. **Sign-coherent at N** iff all K_{G,ch}(N) > 0.
