## Question and SM input
Can positivity of the deformed geometry fix any **dimensionless mass ratio** (m_i/m_j, or the DCI ratios u, v) to discrete values?
SM ledger line: mass ratios (the whole fermion/boson mass spectrum, as ratios).

## Predicted outcome (written before computing)
**CONTINUOUS.**
- Positivity gives only *open* inequalities: 0 < ρρ̃ < 1 and 0 < ηη̃ < 1, hence u > 1 and v > 1.
- Every u ∈ (1, ∞) (and every v) is realised.
- The mass-ratio proxy ρ/ρ̃ (equivalently m₁/m₃ in eq. (22) at fixed u) takes every positive value.
- The face structure is constant on the whole open region, so nothing is discrete.

**Refutation criteria:** "continuous" is refuted if ANY of these holds:
- (a) the allowed set of ρρ̃ (or ρ/ρ̃) on a refining grid stays bounded in count (isolated points);
- (b) the region is empty on an open interval of r ∈ (0, 1);
- (c) a Lean proof shows the allowed u-set is not the full interval (1, ∞).
