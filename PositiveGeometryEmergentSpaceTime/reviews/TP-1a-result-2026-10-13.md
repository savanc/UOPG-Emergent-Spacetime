## Proved
- (a) `x_pos`: sin(kπ/(n+1)) > 0 for 1 ≤ k ≤ n.
- (b) `eigen`: x_k + x_{k+2} = 2cos(π/(n+1))·x_{k+1} for every k. With `x_zero` and `x_end` (x₀ = x_{n+1} = 0), this is the path-adjacency eigen-equation at all n nodes, ends included.
- (c) eigenvalue 2cos(π/h), h = n+1. `eigenvalue_coxeter` is only a cast restatement and adds no content.
- (d) `ratio21`: x₂/x₁ = 2cos(π/(n+1)) for n ≥ 1. `golden_A4`: for A₄ this ratio is the golden ratio φ, consistent with SG1 `two_cos_pi_div_five`.
- Numeric: for A1–A8, A x = λx to 1e−16, λ_PF = 2cos(π/(n+1)), and the ratios match the PF vector to 1e−14 (consistent with SG-1 G1).

Honest scope:
