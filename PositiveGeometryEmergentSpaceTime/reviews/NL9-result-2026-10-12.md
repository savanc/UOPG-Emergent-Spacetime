# NL9 — blind positivity test of the nine-loop six-gluon MHV amplitude: WELL-POSEDNESS FAIL (pre-data)

Date: 2026-10-12. Status: **red / NOT POSED**. No data file downloaded; no pre-registration committed (the gate failed first).

## 1. The object (read from metadata only: Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.22949278 record JSON, Cosmic9 data page, Anthropic post) [CITED]
- Planar N=4 SYM, **six-gluon MHV**, **L = 9**, BDS-like normalised amplitude E^(9)(u,v,w) (conventions of arXiv:1903.10890 / 2308.08199). Not an integrand: a **weight-18 hexagon function**.
- Released as (a) its **symbol** (424 quintuple coproducts over the weight-13 hexagon space, mod two 31-bit primes; septuple file MHV9septuples, exact), (b) the **function** as 8,028 weight-11 septuple components plus zeta-valued completion and 72 integration constants (MHV9septuples_function.zip, 94 MB zipped / 296 MB, Mathematica-style tables referring to SixGluonAmpsAndCops.m), (c) the 3-point form factor at 9 loops, Δ=0 data, flux-tube data. Zenodo holds the 8 big files (≈5 GB total).
- Function computed once, no independent second computation; it assumes symbol-level septuple relations lift to function level (stated on the page).

## 3. Well-posedness gate
1. **Pre-statable sign prediction:** yes — sign(E^(9)) = sign((−1)^9 × convention) throughout the positive region, convention-independently: sign(E^(9)/E^(8)) = −1 at every positive-region point.
2. **Computable with our tools:** NO for any generic point. The data page itself says a generic point needs "a numerical evaluation of hexagon functions"; we have no weight-11→18 hexagon-function evaluator (no Mathematica, no PolyLogTools/HyperInt-scale stack), CPU-only box. On the lines (u,u,1), (1,v,v), (u,1,1) everything reduces to HPLs, but this means re-implementing the integrate-up of 8,028 septuples through 7 weights from Mathematica-format tables — a multi-day engineering project with its own unverified assumption, not a rung.
3. **Blind:** NO for the one cheap point. The data page (read in step 1 for metadata) prints E^(9)(1,1,1) = 673807654.73… and E^(9)/E^(8)(1,1,1) = −14.11; (1,1,1) is in the positive region. So that check was seen before any pre-registration.
4. **Discrimination:** a single sign at one point is 1 bit (random pass 50%); alternation was already established through 5 loops, so prior expectation of pass is high.
→ **FAIL: not posed.** Recorded red.

## 4. Incidental, non-blind observation [SIMULATED? no — CITED value, not run]
E^(9)(1,1,1)/E^(8)(1,1,1) = −14.11 < 0: consistent with loop-by-loop sign alternation at (1,1,1). Absolute sign (+ at L=9) appears opposite to a naive (−1)^L reading; most likely a normalisation/convention difference between 1611.08325 and the 2308.08199 BDS-like E — UNRESOLVED, not claimed either way.
