## Stage 1: the normalisation is verified [SIMULATED, ≥ 20 digits]

**Route A** (`scripts/ht5g_routeA.py`, `ht5g_stage1.py`) is an independent route with no fiber, no
det(VᵀV), no Y-chart and no Grothendieck formula.
- The Lee/Haar measure is written in the Ising chart of a top network N = tree + extra edges.
- The extra edges are sent to J_e → 0 (an iterated residue). The result is divided by the tree carrier.
- Derivatives are central differences at dps 80 with h = 10⁻³⁵, cross-checked at h = 10⁻²⁵.
- The limit uses ε = 10⁻²⁵, cross-checked at 10⁻³⁰.
- The value is cross-checked across 2–4 different top completions N per tree.

**Results:**

| | R_res(H-T5f) / J_A | spread |
|---|---|---|
| n = 8 (route C control, 2 trees × 4 points) | 1 | 3.7 · 10⁻²² |
| n = 10 (5 trees × 4 points) | 1 | 6.1 · 10⁻²¹ |

**Caveats:**
- Only completions with a simple pole in **each** extra edge are valid charts. This was found by a per-edge ε-scaling
  filter, added after the first scan picked degenerate completions; it is disclosed.
- Even after the filter, some completions disagree at some points (T4: 1 of 4; T5: 1 to 3 of 6 evaluations). The
  reported value is the consensus, agreeing to < 10⁻¹⁵ across at least 3 independent evaluations, always including
  ≥ 2 distinct charts.
- Route A is not interval-certified.

**Conclusion:** the H-T5f normalisation was right. The n = 10 Part-A mismatch was not a normalisation error.

## Stage 2: the vertex-chain rule. PASS (constant value not as predicted) [SIMULATED, spread 5 · 10⁻²⁵]

- **Rule V**, HWX J2 extended to chains:

  J_V = Σ_{S ⊆ internal vertices} Π_{edges with exactly one end in S} sech 2J_e

- **Result:** Ω_tile = 32 · carrier · J_V on all 5 n = 10 trees × 8 points, with spread 5.2 · 10⁻²⁵.
- **Alternative P** (pairwise only, no 3-chain term) FAILS: spread 0.13.
- **Constant:** universal and a power of 2, so PASS under the prereg. However, the predicted 2^{k−1} = 16 was
  **wrong**: it is 32.
- **Across n:** together with n = 8 (constant 8), the pattern is 2^{n−5} (= 2^{2k−5}). This is a two-point
  observation [CONJECTURE].

### Why H-T5f failed [PROVED by bookkeeping]

H-T5f's n = 10 "omit" test used H-T5d's network R1b. R1b is a star with two triangles: it has bounded faces, so it
is **not a tree**. On the genuine Catalan tree T5 the rule holds.

R1b alone is still off under V (spread 0.60) [SIMULATED]. It needs the face-loop terms J1/J3 of HWX, which have not
been tested.

## Plain words

Each tree-shaped Ising network is one piece of the ABJM amplituhedron. On that piece the true ABJM measure is:

(a constant 8 or 32) × (the simple "Ising carrier" Π dlog sinh 2J) × (a correction factor J).

The correction factor J is a sum over every set of internal spins: each set contributes Π sech 2J over the bonds
that leave the set. This is exactly HWX's 2014 loop rule written in Ising variables, with shared corners dropped.

The earlier n = 10 "failure" came from testing a network that was not a tree.

## Novelty [CITED / SPECULATION]

- **CITED:** the rule is HWX 1402.1479 §2.4.2 (J1/J2).
- **New here (as far as we know, unverified), each a numerical check:**
  - the Ising-variable form of the rule, as a "cut-set" sum Σ_S Π_{∂S} sech 2J;
  - its numerical confirmation on BCFW tiles of the momentum amplituhedron at n = 8 and n = 10;
  - the 2^{n−5} constants.
- The cut-set form resembles a high-temperature / boundary expansion [SPECULATION]. We do not claim a structural
  meaning.
