## What this changes in the mission

- **Kill criterion 1 is not triggered.** (1,3) signature comes out of K1 (complex 2×2 matrices / the Gr(2,4) chart) once we pick the Hermitian real structure. [PROVED]
- **Plücker coordinates are now derived, not assumed.** They are the minors of [I | X], and the Klein relation is a theorem. [PROVED]
- **The tension with positivity is now sharp.** Real Minkowski points are *Hermitian* X, i.e. complex entries with a reality condition. The positive Grassmannian Gr⁺(2,4) needs *real* matrices with positive minors, which gives split signature (2,2). So the two real slices of complex Gr(2,4) are different, and no positivity statement about Minkowski space follows from M1. This is the kill-criterion-1 caveat: positivity may live only in the particle/amplitude layer (M0′, M2). [CONJECTURE, not proved either way]

## What remains open (not claimed)

1. **The twistor reality structure.** The statement that Hermitian X ⟺ lines in twistor space ℂ⁴ that are null for the (2,2) Hermitian form Φ (lines in PN) is not formalised. We have the chart for all X and, separately, the Hermitian condition, but not the Φ-isotropy characterisation.
2. **Points at infinity.** The chart covers only p₀₁ ≠ 0. Compactified Minkowski space and the full Klein quadric aren't done.
3. **Surjectivity** of SL(2,ℂ) onto the restricted Lorentz group (so that it is a true double cover) isn't proved here, and I didn't find it in Physlib.
4. **Linking `chart` to Mathlib's `Module.Grassmannian`**, so that "a point of Gr(2,4)" is Mathlib's object and not just a 2×4 matrix of rank 2.
5. **Positivity (M2) and particles (M0′)** are untouched.
