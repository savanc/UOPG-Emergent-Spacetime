## Part B: adjacency vs associahedron

| n | Tree tiles | Wall edges (from degenerations) | Flip edges (prereg bijection) | Equal? | Internal / external codim-1 walls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 2 | 1 | 1 | **yes** | 1 / 8 |
| 10 | 5 | 5 (pentagon) | 5 | **yes** | 5 / 25 |
| 12 | 14 | 21 | 21 (K₅ 1-skeleton) | **yes** | 21 / 84 |

**Verdict: PASS at n = 8, 10, 12** [PROVED-by-exact-computation, combinatorial].
- Every codim-1 boundary cell of a tree tile is shared by at most 2 tiles.
- The shared ones are exactly the flips of the dual polygon triangulation.
- At n = 10 the H-T5d wall list is reproduced (now derived independently of flips), and those walls carry certified
  spurious-pole cancellation (H-T5d (c)).
- **Caveat:** at n = 12 this is OG≥0 cell combinatorics only. That shared OG boundaries map to amplituhedron walls is
  Perlstein–Tessler Thm 5.0.3 (injectivity on boundary strata) [CITED]. No n = 12 Y-space test was run (6-dim fibers:
  not fit for the box this rung).

**Prior art:** Perlstein–Tessler Cor. 4.3.5 already shows that each internal boundary of a BCFW cell is shared by
exactly two cells, which are the two resolutions of a square in the triangle tree, i.e. a flip. So "BCFW adjacency =
associahedron graph" is essentially known [CITED]. Our result is an independent exact check, plus the explicit
dictionary to Ising tree networks.

## Part A: carrier

**Pre-registered verdicts:**

| Hypothesis | n = 8 | n = 10 |
|---|---|---|
| H0: pure carrier, J = 1 (\|R\| constant) | **FAIL** (spread 0.56) | **FAIL** (spread 0.69) |
| H1 as encoded: C1 V-faces, shared edge counted once | **FAIL** (spread 0.25) | **FAIL** (spread 0.53) |

So per the prereg, Part A is **H2: neither**. The tile form is **not** the pure carrier pushforward: loop-free networks
still carry a nontrivial J.

**Post-hoc [SIMULATED, POST-HOC — not a prereg verdict]:**
- At n = 8 the rule exactly as written in H-T3b fits to 22 digits. That rule omits the sech of an edge whose two
  endpoints are both selected V-faces ("a corner shared by two same-type touching faces is omitted"). The prereg's H1
  mis-encoded it as "counted once".
- With that rule, |R/J| = 8.000… (spread 7·10⁻²²) on both n = 8 trees:
  **Ω_tile = 8 · carrier · J_C1 pushforward**, with J_C1 = 1 + sech 2J_{1a} sech 2J_{2a} sech 2J_{ab}
  + sech 2J_{ab} sech 2J_{3b} sech 2J_{4b} + sech 2J_{1a} sech 2J_{2a} sech 2J_{3b} sech 2J_{4b}.
- The same rule **does not** fit n = 10 (spread 0.52; it was tested on n = 10 after fixing it from n = 8).
- So the J of a 3-vertex chain needs more (e.g. a different treatment of non-adjacent vertex pairs). Alternatively,
  part of the n = 10 3-fold residue normalisation (det(VᵀV), chart) has a point-dependent factor we have not
  validated. The n = 10 pipeline's ratios across walls were validated in H-T5d (c), but its absolute normalisation was
  not.
- **n = 10 status: OPEN.**

**Signs** [SIMULATED]:
- On every tile, sign R = sign det(∂Φ/∂J) · sign Ω_res with a fixed overall convention.
  - n = 10: R > 0, det > 0, and Ω_res > 0 on all 5 tiles (40/40 points).
  - n = 8: R < 0 and det < 0 on both tiles, with Ω_res > 0.
- So the network chart's orientation, combined with the positive Lee residue, gives each tile a single sign. The
  orientation is uniform across the tiles of one n.

**Amplitude statement:** we cannot claim "amplitude = Σ tree tiles of pure-carrier pushforwards". That is false at
n = 8 and n = 10 (H0 FAIL). At n = 8 the post-hoc identity gives amplitude form = 8 · Σ_tiles pushforward(carrier ·
J_C1), conditional on BCFW = amplitude (HW, CITED).

## Plain words

- **Does the pure carrier suffice on trees?** No. Even loop-free Ising networks need a J correction from their
  interior vertices. At n = 8 that correction is exactly HWX's V-face rule (the H-T3b C1 form). At n = 10 we have not
  found it yet.
- **Is tile adjacency the associahedron?** Yes. The tree tiles meet exactly along flips: a pentagon at n = 10 and the
  21-edge K₅ graph at n = 12. Perlstein–Tessler essentially already show this; we confirmed it exactly and in Ising
  language.

## Novelty

- Part B: known in substance (PT Cor. 4.3.5); the Ising dictionary and the n = 12 enumeration are a check.
- Part A: the negative result (J ≠ 1 on trees) is new to this project. The n = 8 identification is HWX's rule [CITED],
  found post hoc.
