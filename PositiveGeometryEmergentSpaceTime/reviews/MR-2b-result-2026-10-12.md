- In terms of LECs (CITED from memory of Gasser–Leutwyler 1984 one-loop C(s,t,u), NOT re-read): the ℓ̄1/ℓ̄2 terms

## Gate
1. Lattice-only reconstruction: **NO.** ℓ̄1+ℓ̄2 is not a lattice-determined combination (FLAG has ℓ̄1−ℓ̄2 only);
   b̄5, b̄6 need r_i from resonance saturation/phenomenology. Any reconstruction would import CGL/BCEGS inputs
   → not independent of MR-2.
2. Two independent lattice sources: **NO** for ℓ̄1+ℓ̄2. A workaround (ℓ̄1+2ℓ̄2 from lattice a0², combined with
   lattice ℓ̄1−ℓ̄2) needs ℓ̄3, ℓ̄4 and two-loop r_i in the inversion and rests on very few ℓ̄1−ℓ̄2 determinations;
   it is one composite chain, not two sources. SPECULATION that its error would exceed the 1/3 band.
3. Alternatives:
   - I=2 channel alone: the zero is a statement about A(P); T²(P) = A(t,s,u)+A(u,t,s) evaluates A at other
     points, so I=2 data do not test the zero. Not posed.
   - Mass condition 4a1+2a3+a4=0: same LEC content (contains ℓ̄1+ℓ̄2-type pieces); same failure.
   - M_π dial (heavier-pion ensembles): P is subthreshold (s=M², t=M², u=2M² < 4M²), unreachable by Lüscher at any
     M_π; and the M_π dependence of R(P) is fixed by chiral logs once ℓ̄i are given, so "the zero tracks M²"
     is ChPT structure, not an independent test (CONJECTURE-level argument, not computed).
4. Random-point pass fraction: same normaliser as MR-2 → f ≈ 0.35 (SIMULATED in MR-2); would still apply.
