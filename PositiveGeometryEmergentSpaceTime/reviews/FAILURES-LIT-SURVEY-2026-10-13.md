## 2. Is the failure itself known or publishable? [ASSESS]
- **Positivity forces m_π=0 / the trajectory-splitting condition:** known since the 1970s [CITED Pramana 1978; 2002.05419]. Not novel.
- **LS vs KSRF factor:** known "voodoo QCD" closeness without equality [CITED 2203.11950; hep-ph/9612486]. Not novel.
- **M3 miss:** a standard scalar-sector effect on L3 [CITED 0812.2631; hep-ph/9612486]. Not novel. Worth one paragraph in a write-up.
- **MR-6 no-go (zero never inside a decay region):** I have not seen it stated for hidden zeros [CONJECTURE]. It's a small, possibly publishable Lean-verified remark, alongside 2601.16860.
- **LAT-2/3 "lattice fits impose the Adler zero":** explicitly acknowledged by the dispersive community [CITED 2302.08443; 2304.03762]. Not novel.
