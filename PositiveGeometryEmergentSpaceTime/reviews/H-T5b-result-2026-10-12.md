## Deviations (disclosed)

1. **F-unif box.** Rejection from [−1, 1]⁶ had acceptance 1.5·10⁻⁵ (too slow). By GP, the positive region lies in
   (0, 1)⁶, so rejection from [0, 1]⁶ gives the *same* distribution (acceptance 2.5·10⁻³). Prefilter in floats, final
   acceptance exact.
2. **Measure in (c).** The prereg wrote Π_{i=1..8} M_i. On OG, M_{i+4} = M_i, so that would square every pole. Lee/HW
   use Π_{i=1..k}, and that was used.
3. Sign-flip rejection on Gr(4,6) was already ruled out in the prereg (the OMA has codimension 3).

## Plain words

The H-T5 doubt was a solver artefact. With an exact inverse, every sampled point of the n = 8 amplituhedron lies in
exactly one of HW's two BCFW cells σ1, σ2. The same holds for the cyclic-shift pair. Adding the two tiles' forms
cancels the pole on the wall between them.

In Ising terms, both HW tiles are N1 with one boundary bond cut ((1,2) or (2,3)), and their shared wall is N1 with both
cut [PROVED for the sample points; general statement CONJECTURE]. The cyclic tiles are N1 with (2,c) cut vs (4,c)
fused, and their wall is N1 with both. So the Ising picture describes the tiling cleanly, but it still **does not
select** it: the 0-vs-∞-on-one-edge rule from H-T5 (d) stays FAILED. The tiling is "cut two neighbouring bonds of one
network", which is BCFW's choice re-expressed, not derived.

## Caveats

- Sampling-based. Coverage is a statistical bound (0.18%), not a proof of tiling.
- (c) was tested on one path per wall, and only relative cancellation was checked. It was **not** compared against
  the known 8-point amplitude.
- The orientation (sign) of each tile form comes from the residue formula, not from an independent convention.
