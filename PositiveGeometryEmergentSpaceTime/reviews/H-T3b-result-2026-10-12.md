## (a) Exact n = 3 [SIMULATED, exact symbolic]

Script: `scripts/ht3b_symbolic_n3.py`, output in `scripts/ht3b_symbolic_n3_output.txt`.

Method:
- In G2 the OG block is c ∈ O(3) (c cᵀ = I is checked symbolically).
- Lee's δ-measure is therefore Haar: |det(cᵀ∂_a c)_{i<j}|.
- Everything is rational in t = tanh L.

Results:
- **Star:** R_Y = −(1 + Π sech 2L_i), exactly.
- **Triangle:** R_Δ = 1 + Π tanh 2K_ij, exactly.
- The relative sign is the orientation. The overall constant differs from H-T3's coarea ⅛ only by the Haar
  normalisation.
- **Star–triangle:** det(∂ log sinh 2K/∂ log sinh 2L) = −J_Y/J_Δ holds as an identity of rational functions of the
  triangle couplings. The proof uses the implicit-function form, with t_i² = m_ij m_ik/m_jk and P² = m₁₂m₁₃m₂₃.

## (c) and (d): the general rule and the blind n = 4 test

Script: `scripts/ht3b_n4_rule.py`, output in `scripts/ht3b_n4_rule_output.txt`.

**Rule C1** (fixed before testing):

J = Σ_S Π_{F∈S} Π_{corners of F not shared within S} w,

where:
- each interior Ising vertex v gives a closed medial face with weight w = Π_{e∋v} sech 2J_e;
- each bounded interior Ising face f gives one with weight w = Π_{e∈∂f} tanh 2J_e;
- S ranges over collections of closed faces, no two of which share a medial edge;
- a corner shared by two same-type faces in S (they touch at one medial crossing) is omitted.

**Networks tested.** Three reduced n = 4 top-cell networks, each with 6 edges, τ = (15)(26)(37)(48) and 6 crossings
(checked exactly):
- **N1:** 1 V-face and 2 F-faces touching at a corner.
- **N2:** 2 V-faces touching at a corner and 1 F-face.
- **N3:** 1 V-face and 2 F-faces touching at a corner.

All three have 3 closed faces, with both edge-adjacency and corner-touching present.

**Results:**
- 2⁴ · R = C1 to all 18 printed digits, at 4 random points × 2 choices of j × 3 networks.
- The normalisation 2^{−k} found at k = 2 and k = 3 persists at k = 4.
- C2 (shared corner squared) and C3 (Π(1 + w)) miss by 0.1–23%.
- **Consistency:** N1 and N2 tuned to the same OG point (by matching correlations) give
  det(∂ log sinh 2J₂/∂ log sinh 2J₁) = −1.55507517295 = −R₁/R₂ to 12 digits.

**Verdict: PASS for C1.** HWX's printed J1 + J2 rule, rewritten in Ising variables, reproduces the Lee measure exactly
at k = 4. J3-type configurations (two faces sharing two corners without an edge) do not occur in these networks, so
that part of HWX's rule is untested.

## Plain words

- Over a planar Ising network, the measure ABJM uses is the product of dlog sinh 2J over the edges, times a
  correction J.
- J is a sum over sets of non-adjacent "loops":
  - each interior spin contributes the product of sech 2J over its edges;
  - each interior face contributes the product of tanh 2J around it;
  - a shared corner is dropped.
- So the carrier isn't invariant on its own. Carrier × J is, and J is entirely built from the network's own couplings.
  The modulation (kinematics) then gives the amplitude.
- The rule is HWX's. We wrote it in Ising language, checked it independently from Lee's measure at 8 points, and
  proved the n = 3 move identity in Lean.

## Is it new?

- The rule itself is not: it is HWX 2014.
- Plausibly new, though small:
  - the explicit Ising-coupling form, which answers GP §9's request at n ≤ 4;
  - the first independent check of HWX's J against Lee's measure at k = 4, via a route that doesn't use on-shell
    gluing;
  - the first machine-checked proof of HWX (2.55).
- A closed form is not found. For example, J is not a determinant or Pfaffian, or at least none was tested. The rule
  remains combinatorial.

## Caveats

- Only top cells were tested. Lower cells need residues of Lee's measure.
- n = 4 rests on 40-digit numerics, not symbolic proof.
- The G2 gauge convention is an input, carried over from H-T3, Amendment A1.
- J3/J13/J23 are untested.
- The Lean proof covers the algebraic identity, not the measure derivation.
