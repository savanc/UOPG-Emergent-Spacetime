## 3. Proved vs hypothesised vs cited

- **PROVED (Lean):** moments of a positive measure ⇒ Hankel PSD (any size, point masses; 2×2 for
  measures) ⇒ `f0 f2 ≥ f1²`, `det H₃ ≥ 0`; support in [0, xmax] ⇒ gap. Positivity is used
  essentially: the signed counterexamples are proved to violate.
- **HYPOTHESIS (CITED, not proved):** that forward coefficients *are* such moments. This needs
  analyticity, s↔u crossing, `A/s² → 0`, and `Im A ≥ 0` (Bellazzini et al. eqs. (2)–(7);
  EFT-hedron §3, §6), plus a tree-level/weak-coupling matching `a_n = c_{2n+2}` (eq. (30)).
- **CITED:** toy amplitudes T1–T4 (formulas from the papers, numerics ours).
- **Not established:** the converse (every PSD Hankel sequence is a moment sequence — Hausdorff /
  Stieltjes) is cited, not proved. Our measure-level Hankel is 2×2 only; the n×n result is for
  finite point-mass measures (which is what tree-level UV completions give, Bellazzini eq. (33)).

## 4. Link to M2

M2's `secondDeriv_nonneg_of_dispersion` at s = u = 0 has curvature
`A''(0) = (2/π)∫_{s0}^∞ (Im_s + Im_u)(x)/x³ dx`: exactly `2·f0` for the same positive density
(Bellazzini eq. (3), n = 0, Im_s = Im_u by crossing). So M2 proved the 0-th face (f0 ≥ 0); M2b proves
the first non-linear face of the same object. Not formalised as a single Lean statement (the series
expansion of the kernel was out of scope); it is a documented identity.

## 5. What this shows, and what it does not

Shows: the moment-curve parabola `v = u²` — the first piece of the EFT-hedron's positive geometry —
**emerges** from `Im A ≥ 0` alone. A single resonance sits on it; adding resonances moves into the
interior (convex hull of points on the moment curve); a gap adds the upper line. This is "positive
geometry derived from unitarity" in the smallest honest case, with positivity as a hypothesis about
the measure and the geometry as a theorem.

Does **not** show: anything about pion data (pre-registered: no data check; needs O(p⁸) and arcs),
anything about our Gr⁺ structure, or anything about nature beyond consistency of known string /
scalar amplitudes. The checks were not risky predictions: once the inputs are moment sequences, the
inequalities are theorems. Their value is as transcription guards and as Lean infrastructure.
