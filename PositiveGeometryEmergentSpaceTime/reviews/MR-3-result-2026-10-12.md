- Albert–Rastelli 2203.11950: chiral limit, m_π = 0 throughout ("straightforward to generalize to non-zero quark mass").
  Bound on g̃ρ² = g²_{ππρ}/(2 g₁₀ m_ρ²) = g² f_π²/m_ρ² vs gap M'/m_ρ; plateau "close to (not equal to) KSRF".
  LS is in the interior of their (g̃₂, g̃₂') plot, g̃₂ = ln 2 (eq. 75) — we reproduce this exactly (normalisation check).
- Fernandez–Pomarol–Riva–Sciotti 2211.12488: large-N positivity; kinks ↔ single light pole; scalar/vector-less LS and Coon
  deformations near boundaries; couplings fall with spin (explains VMD). Ratios bounded: chiral-Lagrangian coefficients and
  meson couplings in units of f_π, m_ρ.
- Guerrieri–Penedones–Vieira 2011.02802: massless pions, bounds on the two leading chiral Wilson coefficients (no m_ρ prediction).
  Massive-pion S-matrix bootstrap (Guerrieri et al. 2018, cited from memory) takes m_π as the unit/input.
- Bianchi–Consoli–Di Vecchia 2002.05419: N-pion LS from the NS model with the tachyon shifted to a *massless* pion; Adler zeros,
  NLSM limit α'→0 at fixed F_π. Not a massive-pion LS (correction to the task brief).
- Massive LS with Lovelace's Adler condition α_s+α_u = 1 at s=u=m_π²: Lovelace 1968 / Shapiro 1969 (from memory, not fetched).
**What the literature bounds:** f_π-normalised couplings/LECs at fixed m_ρ, always with m_π = 0 or m_π an input. Nobody claims m_π/m_ρ.

## 2. Well-posedness (decided pre-data)
- PROVED (`adler_condition`, `rho_condition`): Adler zero + ρ on the leading trajectory leave a one-parameter family in r = m_π²/m_ρ²
  (α' = 1/(2(1−r)), α₀ = (1−2r)/(2(1−r))). Level-1 positivity only needs r < 1/4 (`level1_decomp`, `level1_positive`) — i.e. threshold.
- PROVED (`level2_decomp`, `level2_scalar_negative`, `positivity_forces_chiral`): at the f₂ level the J=0 Legendre coefficient is
  r(2r−1)/(4(r−1)²) < 0 for 0<r<1/2. Positivity therefore forces r = 0 inside the minimal family. (At r=0 this is the
  MR-1c f₂-level scalar zero; any pion mass pushes it negative — a ghost scalar.)
- SIMULATED: exact rational scan r ∈ [0, 0.2475] step 1/400, levels n ≤ 8, all Legendre coefficients: only r = 0 survives.
- CONJECTURE: deformed families (satellite terms, non-linear trajectories, FPRS-type) restore positivity for a continuum of r, as
  bootstrap work with m_π as an input implies. Not constructed here.
- Verdict: the ingredients give "m_π/m_ρ = 0" (minimal family) or no constraint (deformed) — neither is a window. Physically m_π² ∝ m_q
  is an external parameter of QCD, so CONJECTURE: no geometry-only kinematic mechanism can fix it without a quark-mass-like input.
  m_π/m_ρ ≈ 0.18 was known to us (memory) and was not scored.
