## Corrections to the brief, kept on the record

1. The brief said "odd-n amplitudes vanish in the limit". They do not. NLSM has no odd-point amplitudes,
   but the shifted 5-point Tr(φ³) is not zero. It goes as δ⁻², and its leading coefficient L5 is a
   **mixed** pion/scalar amplitude. Under the pion normalisation δ^{n−2} = δ³ it would actually diverge.
   The shift is designed for even n: for odd n it cannot keep every c_ij fixed (here c14 moves).
   This is what [H] and [N] say, and the pre-registration was rewritten to test it.
2. The field-theory δ-limit is in 2401.05483, not 2312.16282.

## What this shows

- **PROVED:** at n = 4, 5, 6, one function — the canonical form of the M2c associahedron, extended to n = 6 —
  turns into the leading-order pion amplitudes under a fixed linear shift of kinematic space. The 4- and
  6-point NLSM amplitudes come out exactly, hidden zeros included. Soft-pion (Adler) behaviour follows
  from the skinny-rectangle zeros together with the absence of X_{e,e}/X_{o,o} poles.
- **First contact with real particle data, at LO:** the flavour-ordered output ∝ s + t assembles into
  Weinberg's chiral-limit ππ amplitude, A ∝ s. Two inputs are needed for the data step: Weinberg's mass
  insertion A = (s − M²)/F² and the measured F_π, M_π. With them, the scattering lengths land within the
  pre-registered 30%.

## Limits (as weighty as the result)

- **The shift is an input rule, not derived.** [H] §7 and [AF] motivate it (stringy u-variables;
  "circles and triangles"), but we pick the parity assignment. Its justification is that it works.
- **No mass and no scale.** The geometry is massless. f_π² = δ is free, and M_π enters only through
  Weinberg's hand insertion. So **D2 tests ChPT at LO, not our geometry.** Any LO chiral Lagrangian passes it.
- **D2 is not blind and does not discriminate.** The 30% tolerance comes from CGL's own chiral series.
  a0⁰ sits at −28%, near the edge, and is 4.4σ away from the measurement on experimental errors alone.
  That gap is the well-known O(p⁴) correction (CGL: 0.159 → 0.200 → 0.216). The LO ratio a0²/a0⁰ = −2/7
  vs the measured −0.195 shows the same.
- **Pions are composite Goldstone bosons, not fundamental SM particles.** Tree level only. Loops ([N] §II)
  are cited, not checked. U(N) flavour ordering, planar.
- No GeV is derived, and nothing here touches M3–M6.
