## Verdict (plain language)

**NO-GO, as a precise theorem (a PASS under the pre-registered rule). Kill criterion #1, second clause, FIRES.**

- **No real Minkowski point is positive in the standard order.** In the standard column order, positivity in the
  sense of Gr⁺(2,4) never holds at a real Minkowski point, not even at a single one.
- **In any order or sign twist, positive Minkowski points are confined to a hyperplane.** They lie in the
  hyperplane y = 0 (a 3D (1,2) slice) and never fill an open region of spacetime.
- **The closure touches Minkowski space only on light rays.** In the standard order, the closure Gr≥0 meets real Minkowski
  space in exactly two null half-rays.
- **Wick rotation only reaches positivity at the split end.** Along the rotation that connects the two real forms,
  positivity appears only at the split (2,2) end.
- **Even planar momentum invariants fail in real (1,3).** Moving positivity from spacetime points to the planar
  momentum invariants of M2c does not rescue it for real (1,3) kinematics. At five particles, real Lorentzian momenta
  (in any dimension) can never make all planar invariants positive, in either sign convention. With six particles the
  "Euclidean" sign pattern is reachable. In split signature both five-particle patterns are reachable.
  *Forward note 2026-10-20 (H1, approved by author approved (2026-10-20)): "positive" here means **strictly** of one sign (Lean `no_euclid5`,
  `no_pos5`, `no_pos4` use `<`). The boundary case, all X ≥ 0 with some X = 0 (the closed `Assoc.region`), is
  **open**. Lean is D = 4; any D is the paper argument below. The text above stays as written (superseded where it reads
  wider).*

So positivity in this programme is a property of the *analytically continued* amplitude: of complex or split
kinematics, where amplitudes are standardly computed before continuation. It is not a property of real spacetime
points, and at n = 5 it is not even a property of real Lorentzian momenta. This matches what the field already
does (Witten; Arkani-Hamed–Trnka; Cachazo–Mizera–Zhang; ABMRS). We now have it as checked theorems with exact
boundaries.

## What kind of bridge exists

1. **Not a point bridge.** Real Minkowski points carry no positivity in the standard order, and in any convention
   they carry it at most on the measure-zero (1,2) slice y = 0 (B1). The only contact is the null-ray
   skeleton of the closure. That is a pretty but lower-dimensional fact: the light cone is where the two real forms'
   positivity boundaries touch.
2. **An analytic-continuation bridge only.** det and the null quadric are common holomorphic data (B2). Positivity
   is an open condition on one real form, reached by a Wick rotation, and never at the Hermitian end. Whatever
   positivity "does" for (1,3) physics, it does through analytic continuation of functions (amplitudes), not
   through the real geometry of (1,3).
3. **The kinematic (M2c) positivity is not the honest (1,3) bridge either.** At n = 4 and 6 the Euclidean pattern is
   physical (a crossing channel), but at n = 5 (and every odd n) the associahedron interior has no real Lorentzian
   point (B3). *[Forward note 2026-10-20, H1, approved by author approved (2026-10-20): "interior" = all planar X strictly of one sign. Lean
   proves n = 5 (and n = 4 for s, t > 0) at D = 4; every odd n and any D are the paper argument. The boundary
   (some X = 0) is open.]* The M2c amplitude statements (factorisation, residues) are identities of rational functions, so they
   remain valid everywhere by continuation. Only the *region* lives off the real Lorentzian locus.

## What this means for "positivity is fundamental"

- In real spacetime: **refuted in the strong form.** No positivity statement about real (1,3) points, and none about
  real (1,3) planar kinematics at odd n.
- In the amplitude layer: **unchanged.** M2–M3 never used real-spacetime positivity. Their proofs are algebraic
  identities and dispersion/positivity of spectral data (M2/M2b, which *is* real-kinematic: forward-limit
  unitarity). Note the two different positivities. M2/M2b positivity (spectral densities ≥ 0) is a real (1,3)
  statement, from unitarity. Gr⁺ and associahedron positivity is a statement about the continued domain.
- Reframing: positivity lives on the kinematic and spectral side, in complexified or split space. Spacetime (1,3) is
  a different real form of the same complex geometry, reached only by continuation. This fits "spacetime is
  emergent" in the weak sense that real spacetime is a slice, not the arena of positivity. It does **not** support
  any claim that positivity *produces* Lorentzian spacetime.

   shape of the region.
4. **"Positivity" means two different things in the ladder.** Spectral positivity (M2/M2b, real kinematics) and
   geometric positivity (Gr⁺, associahedron, continued kinematics). The ladder documents must not conflate them.
   MISSION now says so.
