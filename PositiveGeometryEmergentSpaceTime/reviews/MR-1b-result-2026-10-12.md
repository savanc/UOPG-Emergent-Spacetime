### Deviation (disclosed)

S2 was pre-registered as "the single most precise individual measurement, using its own mass, width and BR".
- The most precise ρ3 BR is BECKER 79 (0.259 +0.018/−0.019), but PDG lists no mass or width of its own for it (it
  uses HYAMS data).
- I therefore used CORDEN 79, the most precise measurement that has all three. It is also the most precise f4 BR.
- So S2 is the **same experiment** for both states. It is independent of the PDG average in its mass and width, but
  CORDEN 79 is *one of the inputs* to the PDG BR average.

## Sensitivity

- **Conventions:** V2 (no barrier), V3 (Blatt–Weisskopf, R = 5 GeV⁻¹) and V4 (LS masses) move R by ≤ 7 %. That is
  smaller than the data errors.
- **Source spread is the dominant issue for ρ3.** The PDG and CORDEN 79 ratios differ by a factor of 1.9, driven by
  the width (161 vs 322 MeV). The two sources straddle the prediction.
- **Band dependence:**
  - **f4, S1:** PASSes even with **no** theory band (z = 0.8 on data and conventions alone).
  - **ρ3, S1:** needs a band of at least about 7 %.

## Plain words

The Lovelace–Shapiro amplitude has one input (the ρ mass). Without seeing the data, I worked out how strongly the
next two resonances on the ρ's Regge line, ρ3(1690) and f4(2050), couple to two pions relative to the ρ: 5/16 and
49/240.

PDG's averages come out at 79 % and 91 % of those values. A single classic experiment gives 149 % and 105 %.

All four sit within the expected large-N_c uncertainty. The f4 agreement is good even without any allowance. This
is the first **blind** quantitative agreement in the ladder, though it is modest: ±30–40 % level physics.

## Novelty [CITED / plausibly new]

- The LS amplitude and its spectrum are classic (Lovelace 1968, Shapiro 1969).
- The level-n residues follow from Γ-function algebra (textbook).
- I did not check whether the ρ3/f4 coupling *ratios* are tabulated in AHRV / AR / FPRS: the relevant lines were
  deliberately not read. **Before any claim, read them now (post-verdict).**
- **Plausibly new (small):** the exact ratios 5/16 and 49/240 with κ = 3/7 and 1/2, Lean-proved, and the blind
  pre-registered two-source test.

## Caveats

- **Only partly independent sources:** S2 is the same 1979 experiment for both states, and one of the PDG BR inputs.
- **The ρ3 sources disagree by a factor of 2:** the PASS reflects a wide band plus straddling data, not precision.
- **Inputs not blind:** the branching ratios were half-remembered.
- **No pole/dispersive couplings** exist for ρ3/f4, so V5 could not be run.
- **Unitarisation not modelled:** the LS higher-level unitarity issues are covered only by the level allowance.
