## Verdict: **2. FAIL stands, but weaker.**

- No arithmetic or mapping error was found.
- Under the pre-registered rule the FAIL is robust to:
  - error correlation, ρ ∈ [−0.8, 0.8];
  - the band, up to ±60 %.
- But the outcome is driven by the **renormalisation-scale convention** and by **chiral order**, and the comparison
  rests on **one source**. A large-N_c tree statement has no μ. The rule's choice μ = 0.77 GeV is what produces the
  miss: at μ ≤ 0.6 GeV the same data PASS the same rule.
- Neither ambiguity was in the error budget. The FAIL is therefore a valid pre-registered outcome, but **weak
  physical evidence against the hidden-zero ray**.

## 2. Error band and propagation

- **Is ±30 % justified?** Only as a convention. 1/N_c ≈ 1/3, and BE14's own L_A = 2L1 − L2 ≈ 0.3 L2. It covers
  neither chiral-order shifts nor μ.
  - The NLO→NNLO shift of the ratio is 1.4, i.e. 70 % of −2.
  - The μ ∈ [0.5, 1] GeV spread is −2.3 to −6.1.
  - So the honest total theory/convention uncertainty is **larger than the band**. [SIMULATED]
- **Does the verdict depend on the band?** The FAIL survives any band up to ±60 % and becomes INCONCLUSIVE at ±62 %.
  So it is not fragile to the band *size*; it is fragile to μ and chiral order, which no band in the prereg covered.
- **Data propagation:**
  - BE14 gives no covariance. The MC scan over correlation ρ ∈ {−0.8, −0.5, 0, 0.5, 0.8} gives FAIL for every ρ.
    The 2σ upper edge ranges from −3.49 to −3.03, always below −2.6. ✓
  - The ratio's non-Gaussianity is small (MC median = linear value). ✓
  - BE14's quoted errors **exclude higher-order uncertainties** (stated by BE14). The prereg used them as the full
    data error. **Finding:** that understates the data error. It was disclosed in the M3 review ("What remains
    assumed" 4) but not budgeted.

## 3. Mapping

- **Geometry → −2: re-derived ✓.**
  - Hidden zero at s + u = 0 ⇔ g2,1 = g2,0 (Lean `hidden_zero_iff`).
  - sympy re-derivation: LS O(p⁴) = 2 ln2 (s² + u²) + 4 ln2 su (α′ = 1, dropping π), so g2,1 = g2,0 = 2π ln 2. ✓
  - AR map g2,0 = (2L′3 + 4L′4)/f⁴ and g2,1 = 4L′4/f⁴ give L′3 = 0 (Lean `su3_iff`).
  - The SU(3) renaming L2 = L′4 and L3 = L′3 − 2L′4 (with L′1 = L′2 = 0) gives L3/L2 = −2 (Lean `su3_rename`).
  - SU(2): 2ℓ1 + ℓ2 = 0 (Lean `su2_iff`).
  - `LECRatio.lean` has no `sorry`, `native_decide` or custom axioms.
  - Sign convention check: L3 < 0 in data and in the prediction; L2 > 0. Consistent. **No error found.** [PROVED / CITED]
- **Subtleties not budgeted:**
  - **OZI-violating (double-trace) parts:** the map sets L′1 = L′2 = 0 (2L1 = L2). In BE14, L_A = 2L1 − L2 ≈ 0.31 L2.
    M3w showed that keeping L1 (FPRS eqs. 54–55) cuts D1's exclusion from the unitary region from 2.55σ to 1.6σ. The
    same approximation enters M3's ratio, so part of the miss is a large-N_c-approximation artefact of uncertain size.
  - **Resonance saturation and scale:** the identification "tree-level large-N coefficient = L_i^r(μ ≈ m_ρ)" is the
    standard resonance-saturation lore (Ecker et al. 1989), not a theorem. That is the root of the μ problem in §1.
  - **SU(3) vs SU(2):** both point the same way at 0.77 GeV, but they are not independent tests (BE14 uses CGL input).

## 4. Process

**Already flagged and confirmed:**
- The data were seen before the prereg; the rule "the authors' preferred fit" was chosen knowing it would FAIL
  (disclosed).
- The prediction is prior art (PV 1996; Bartsch et al. 2024).

**New process findings:**
1. **No well-posedness check before testing.** M3w was retrofitted a day later. Today's gate would have required it
   first, and it would have flagged that the unbanded datum is outside every unitary large-N theory.
2. **There was no μ or chiral-order sensitivity budget.** μ and the fit order were fixed (good), but there was no
   pre-registered statement of how sensitive the verdict is to these conventions. Today's gate would demand one, and
   the test would likely have been graded at most "convention-dependent".
3. **Single source in the verdict.** The secondaries could not change the verdict by design, so there was no
   replication requirement. Today's standard (the TRADE-SPACE review) favours replicated targets.
4. **The data error excluded higher orders** (see §2).
5. **The band was a convention, not derived** (already disclosed).
6. **No data entered Lean as more than rounded arithmetic** (correctly tagged after the ladder review).

**Not found:**
- no target moving after the result;
- no arithmetic error;
- no tuning.

## Consequences

- **Kill criterion #4 stays recorded** (it cannot be un-fired). It is noted as weak.
- **For future data rungs (adds to the Rung Protocol):**
  - well-posedness first;
  - a pre-registered μ and chiral-order sensitivity budget;
  - ≥ 2 independent sources;
  - a stated treatment of higher-order errors;
  - prefer observables without scale conventions (e.g. resonance couplings or width ratios: MR-1).
