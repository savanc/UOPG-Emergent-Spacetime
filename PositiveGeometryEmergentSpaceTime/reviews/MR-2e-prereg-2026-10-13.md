## Rule

- Band = 2√(σ_R² + Δμ² + Δtr²):

  - Δtr = 0.3·max(|2(2L6−L4)|, |2L8−L5|) (higher-order allowance, MR-4-style 30%).
- Entries with "(–)" systematic are taken as statistical only.
- **PASS** if ≥ 3 of the 4 collaborations satisfy |R| ≤ band. 2 of 4 → INCONCLUSIVE. ≤ 1 → FAIL.
- **Null:** NDA prior L5, L8 ~ N(0, (1/16π²)²), L4, L6 ~ N(0, (1/(3·16π²))²). f = P(|R| ≤ median band). Discriminating if f ≤ 0.3.
