# Referee review: PositiveGeometryEmergentSpaceTime Papers I and II (AutoResearch, 2026-10-29)
Repo state: HEAD == origin/main == a43af75, clean tree. Read only; nothing edited, no builds. P1 = papers/human-agentic-methods-2026-10/tex/main.tex (10 pp), P2 = papers/paper-ii-evidence/tex/main.tex (19 pp).

## Verdict
Both papers are honest, well sourced and unusually careful. The framing is "research ongoing, no SM quantity derived", the meter is 1/87 with 0/73 quantum numbers, and SURF-1' is worded as "not found in our sweep". There is no overclaim of an SM derivation, of zero-parameter success or of credit. All 32 commit hashes checked exist, and their subjects match the claims. The 12 cited Lean theorems all exist and appear in AxiomsAll; the output reports 1155/630, nonstandard=0, sorry_dependent=0. The main problems are stale or inconsistent snapshots between the two papers, one wrong pause-status statement in P2, six visible TODOs in P2's PDF, and per-rung null/rival reporting that a referee will ask for. Both papers are fixable without new research. Recommendation: minor revision for P1 and major revision for P2, mainly for the TODOs and status corrections.

## Top 5 must-fix
1. **P2-F1 (major)**: P2:201 says AutoResearch has been paused "since 2026-10-28". That is wrong. The batch was unpaused at 05107da (28 Oct 20:17), JORD-1 and SURF-1' ran afterwards, and STOP was re-committed at 433cc73 (29 Oct).
2. **X-1 (major)**: Snapshots disagree. P1 has 1027/538 theorems/defs, 104 reviews and 68 blocker rows (at b1ecf77). P2 has 1155/630, 106 reviews and 70 blocker rows (at cb064b7). P1:316 calls 1027 the count "at the current head", which is false at a43af75.
3. **P2-F2 (major)**: Six red TODOs render in the PDF (P2:273, 328, 403, 453, 462, 466). They must be resolved or removed before any circulation.
4. **P2-F5 (major)**: Null rates and rivals are not reported per rung in P2, although the method requires both. For SURF-1', the U(N) null passes at every N, the ghost-c sensitivity is not reported, and the f-coupling rival is missing. For JORD-1, the null (SM type in 1 of 18 configurations) and the Krasnov rival encoding gap are missing.
5. **P1-F3 (minor, verge of major)**: P1:460 lists SURF-1' (a NO-GO/EMPTY verdict) among "Positive results" without the sweep caveat. Reword it as "a proved no-go … not found in our abstract-level sweep".
