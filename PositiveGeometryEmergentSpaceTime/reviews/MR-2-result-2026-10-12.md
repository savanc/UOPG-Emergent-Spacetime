## What it means
The physical (μ-independent) two-loop ππ amplitude respects the massive hidden-zero point to a few % of its natural
O(p⁴) size. This complements M3: M3's scheme-dependent ratio missed at μ = 0.77 GeV; the on-shell observable that
contains the same combination plus the mass terms passes. Reading (CONJECTURE): the M3 miss is a scale-convention
artefact, and the ℓ3/ℓ4-type mass condition 4a1+2a3+a4 = 0 is approximately obeyed.

## Caveats / deviations
1. NOT BLIND: b̄ values seen during the gate read; b̄1+b̄2+b̄3+b̄4 ≈ −0.19 noticed by eye (disclosed in prereg).
2. P is unphysical: access only via ChPT + Roy representations (model-dependent), not a direct measurement.
3. Sources share the BCEGS two-loop formula; S2's error (μ spread) is not a full error. S2 values rely on resonance r_i.
4. Single-trace (large-N_c) assumption cannot be checked in SU(2); absorbed in the 1/3 band.
5. Known ChPT structure contributes: at one loop b1+b2 contains ℓ3 but not ℓ1 (BCEGS App. D), so part of the near-zero is
   standard ChPT (small ℓ3 effects) — prior-art physics, not a geometry discovery.
6. No zero-parameter content: M_π, F_π are inputs. Mass-scale gap unchanged.
7. Deviation from prereg: none in rule; the "z-scores" column above is added for reporting.
