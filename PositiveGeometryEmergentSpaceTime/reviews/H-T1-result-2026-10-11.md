## Verdict

**PASS.** (a), (b), (e1), (e2) and (e3) are proved in Lean. (c) is proved in the pre-registered scope (one
eliminated state, any number of kept states) with the general case CITED (Abramov) plus a paper proof. (d), the
general bound in Lean, is not done.

Plain language:
- The trace-order conjecture that an observer (a trace) never has more entropy rate than what it observes is
  **false as stated**. The true statement counts entropy per step *of the observed system*.
- That step-count conversion is the same single Kac factor 1/π(S) as their "time dilation".
- For reversible chains, the observer's trace is exactly an electrical network's response matrix. Lam's theorem
  places that matrix in the totally nonnegative Grassmannian. We built the point explicitly for 2 and 3 observed
  states and proved its coordinates positive.
- This positivity comes from probabilities and conductances. It is real, so it survives the M1b no-go.

**Nothing here tests "time is the step count", "mass is entropy rate", or any Lorentz claim. These stay
CONJECTURE.**

## The corrected statement of the conjecture

> *Traces* §3 (verbatim): "We also conjecture that if P ≤t Q then the entropy rate of P is less than or equal to the
> entropy rate of Q."

Corrected (Q stationary irreducible with law π; P = Tr_S Q):

> **π(S) · h(P) ≤ h(Q).** Equivalently, per step of *its own* clock, the observer's entropy rate is at most
> h(Q)/π(S), and it can exceed h(Q). Equality holds iff the excursions outside S carry no entropy beyond what the
> visited S-states reveal.

This is a consequence of Abramov's formula: the state process at return times to S is Markov with kernel Tr, and
it is a factor of the induced system. The Lean proof covers |Q \ S| = 1. The original conjecture holds only "per
tick of Q", i.e. after the same Kac factor 1/π(S) that *Traces* §7 calls time dilation. So the two are one fact:
the observer's clock runs at rate π(S), and entropy per observer tick is boosted by up to 1/π(S).

Courtesy: this corrects a conjecture in a preprint (not yet refereed). The authors flagged it as a conjecture, not a
claim. No outreach has been made. The author decides whether and how to communicate it.

## Meaning for the mission

1. **A real-positivity route into Gr≥0 that survives M1b.** M1b showed that Gr⁺ positivity on spacetime points or
   real Lorentzian kinematics fails. Here the positivity is **probability/conductance positivity**, the
   unitarity-type side. Lam's theorem turns it into total nonnegativity of an honest real Grassmannian point. This
   is the first link in the repo from *real* positivity to Gr≥0.
2. **The preprint's "observer window" = trace = response matrix.** This is a precise dictionary for reversible chains on
   circular planar networks with the observed states on the boundary. The observer sees exactly the Dirichlet-to-
   Neumann data. Circular planar networks are determined by it up to electrical equivalence (Curtis–Ingerman–Morrow).
3. **Limits.** It needs reversibility and planarity. Non-reversible chains (most of the trace-order preprint's CA kernels) get the
   Schur identity (e1) but **not** Lam's embedding, because the response matrix is no longer symmetric. Nothing here
   produces amplitudes yet.
