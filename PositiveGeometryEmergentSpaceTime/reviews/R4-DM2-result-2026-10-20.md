## 2. Surprise: denominator 89, not 87
D1 makes a binder `x : BR5p.Inv` an SM variable (because `Inv` carries the couplings `a, λ₃, λ₄, g_s, b` as fields). Any
statement whose conclusion mentions such an `x` is then an SM-structure statement. `BR5p.Inv` also carries the
kinematics, so two statements entered the denominator that v1 (and my prediction) left out:
- `BR5p.dot4_explicit` — a kinematic dot-product formula in `x`; no coupling appears. NON-CONSTRAINING (correct verdict;
  arguably not SM-structure at all).
- `BR5p.built_zero_of_gauge` — coupling relations as **hypotheses** ⇒ the built amplitude vanishes: the SM→zero direction.
  SMUGGLED (correct: SM data in, not out). The v1 prereg had predicted exactly this verdict; v1 excluded it because its
  conclusion names no SM symbol.
Neither is a derivation, so the numerator, meter_Q and the hand result are unaffected. Excluding these two over-inclusions
by hand gives 1/87, the v1 hand count. The prediction "1/87" missed only because I did not foresee that D1 widens S.
A v3 could restrict D1's S-trigger to conclusions that read an SM *field* (`x.a`, …) rather than any mention of `x`;
not proposed unless wanted (it would not change the numerator).
