- **Does:** within the P-EFT branch, positivity is a **conclusion**.
  - The sign of the EFT curvature, and hence l̄₂ ≥ 27/20, follows from positivity of probabilities (Im T ≥ 0) together with locality and causality (analyticity, Froissart).
  - Nature's pion LECs sit on the allowed side.
  - This meets MISSION M2's pass test for the P-EFT branch: "a Lean theorem in which positivity is a conclusion, not a hypothesis, plus a stated data comparison".
- **Precision on "conclusion":** spectral positivity (Im T ≥ 0) is still an input. It is unitarity, i.e. positivity of probabilities. What is derived is positivity of the **EFT coefficient space**. This is exactly the sense of K2 in MISSION: positivity is *derived from* unitarity and causality, not assumed of the geometry.
- **Does not:**

  - derive the QFT axioms;
  - address P-Net or P-Pole.
