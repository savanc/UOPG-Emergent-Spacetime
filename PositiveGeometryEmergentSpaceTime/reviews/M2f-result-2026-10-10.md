## 1. In one paragraph

Give every propagator of the Tr φ³ associahedron the same mass M (the massive ABHY polytope is the massless one
translated by M² [CGW, Jagadale–Laddha]), then apply the M2d δ-shift. The pion limit is the **massive** pion
amplitude. At n = 4 the ordered amplitude is −(X13 + X24 − 2M²) = −c13 = −(s + t − 2M²), and the SU(2) dressing
turns it into exactly **Weinberg's A(s,t,u) = (s − M²)/F²**. At n = 6 it is A6N(X − M²), the massive 6-pion amplitude
of LO ChPT. I checked that independently with Cayley-parametrisation Feynman rules including the
(F²M²/4)Tr(U + U† − 2) mass term. Within the ansatz of constant offsets, the shift alone allows a one-parameter family
A ∝ s − c (the O(p²) form of generalised ChPT). **The massive hidden zeros select c = M²**: at n = 4, and at n = 6
from just two non-adjacent skinny-rectangle zeros. They fix the offsets up to the trivial δ-reparametrisation. The
**structure** s − M² is therefore forced by geometry plus hidden zeros. The **value** of M, and F, remain inputs.

## 4. What is still assumed

- **The ansatz.** Constant offsets on top of the linear δ-shift. Non-constant or X-dependent deformations are not searched.
- **The conditions.** Hidden zeros, or the ChPT target, are imposed. They are geometric (collapse of the massive
  associahedron) but they are a choice of what to require. The c_ij use the physical external mass, so "which M" is
  the pions' own mass, an input.
- **M and F are inputs.** Nothing here gives a GeV value. f_π² ∝ δ is a free normalisation, as in M2d.
- **Physics identification [CITED]:** A6N(X̃) is the LO ChPT 6-pion amplitude ([CGW]; cross-checked by our Cayley
  Feynman rules, SIMULATED). The SU(2) trace coefficients and the isospin projections are script/standard, not in Lean.
- **Script/Lean split** (as declared): (c2), (d1′), (d2′), the n = 6 family and the ablation are exact sympy, not Lean.
  The (d2) Lean proofs use script-found evaluation points and coefficients, but Lean re-checks every step.
- **Tree level, LO, U(N) planar flavour ordering, equal masses.** Pions are composite, not fundamental SM particles.

## 5. What it means for the mission

- **First mass structure from geometry.** Before M2f, mass entered only through Weinberg's hand insertion
  (s − M²). Now the structure comes out of the same associahedron canonical form: translate it rigidly by M²,
  δ-shift it, and require the hidden zeros. The Adler zero becomes its massive analogue (the zero at the formal soft
  point, broken by exactly O(M²) at the naive one). Locality, unitarity and now the chiral mass term all come out of
  one positive-geometry object at LO.
- **The value of the mass is still an input.** The geometry knows that there is a mass M, and where it enters, but
  not how big it is. In the language of the ladder this is structure, not scale (M3), and no GeV is derived.
- **M3 candidate: the dimensionless ratio a0⁰/a0² = −7/2.** It is geometry-fixed in this chain: the Lean
  `ratio_iff` + `hidden_zero4_forces` give −7/2 with no tuned input. M and F cancel; only the SU(2) group theory
  and the hidden-zero condition enter. **But it is already implied** (Weinberg 1966), and it is LO. Against NA48/2
  (−5.14) it is 32% low in magnitude. The reason is known: the O(p⁴) correction to a0⁰, the CGL series
  0.159 → 0.200 → 0.216. So as an M3 target it would be neither new nor accurate. Pre-registering it now, with the data
  already seen, would be post hoc. **Recommendation: do not use −7/2 as the M3 target.** A fair M3 candidate needs a
  number the geometry fixes beyond LO (see next steps).
