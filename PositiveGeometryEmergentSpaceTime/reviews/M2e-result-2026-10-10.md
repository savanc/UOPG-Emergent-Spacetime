## 1. Solution set (k = 4, exact, all 511 non-empty supports of a ∈ ℝ⁹)

| Conditions | Solution components with L ≢ 0 |
|---|---|
| **C1(4) + C2 + C3 (all 6 legs)** | **exactly one**: support = the six (e,e)/(o,o) chords, b = (1, 1, −1, −1, 1, −1), i.e. **a = λ·a\***, λ ≠ 0. λ < 0 includes the mirror. |
| C1(4) + C3, no C2 | the same single line, so C2 is redundant |
| C1(4) + C2, no C3 | 11 components: the a\* line, plus families that also shift the (o,e) chords X14, X25, X36 (dimensions 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 4, 4, 4, 7) |

| C1(4)+C2+C3, legs {1,2,3} | the a\* line only |
| legs {1,2} or {1,2,4} | a\* line + a 1-parameter family on support + X25 |
| legs {1,3,5} | a\* line + three 1-parameter families (two (o,e) chords shifted) |
| k = 2, 3, 5, 6 (C1+C2+C3) | **none** |

Leg subsets up to the dihedral symmetry of the hexagon: every 2-leg set lies inside {1,2}, {1,2,4} or {1,3,5}, so all
fail. Of the 3-leg classes, {1,2,3} works, while {1,2,4} (≅ {1,2,5}) and {1,3,5} fail. **Minimal Adler input: three
consecutive legs.** The legs {1} and {1,4} scans were stopped unfinished. They are not needed, since {1} ⊂ {1,2} and
{1,4} ⊂ {1,2,4}, and each superset already has extra solutions.

**Numeric cross-check.** This check is independent of the Gröbner step. It evaluates δ⁴·A6(X + δa) directly from the 14
cubic diagrams with exact rationals, at δ = 10⁶ and 10⁸. Results:
- a\* and its mirror converge to L ≠ 0 (e.g. 635.915 → 635.936 = L) and have the Adler zero on all six legs.
- A C1-only family member (support + X14) converges to L ≠ 0 but fails the Adler zero on legs 2 and 5.
- A full-support member satisfies all the C1(<4)/C2/C3 coefficient equations, but δ⁴A6 → 0 (as ~δ⁻²): L ≡ 0, so it fails the nonzero clause of C1.

## 2. What is proved where

**Script-verified (exact sympy/Singular, re-runnable; NOT in Lean):**
- The large-δ expansion and the reduction "C1(4), C2, C3 ⇒ polynomial coefficient equations in b = 1/a".
- That 503 of 511 supports die because a coefficient is a nonzero constant or a monomial in b. All 8 survivors shift
  all six (e,e)/(o,o) chords, plus some subset of {X14, X25, X36}.
- The rewriting of each equation in a (numerator under b → 1/a, monomial factors dropped, which is valid because a ≠ 0).
- On the full support, L = P₄ is a linear form in X.

**Lean-proved (`PositiveGeometryEmergentSpaceTime.ShiftForced`, standard axioms only):**
- `forced_main`: on the six-chord support, 5 coefficient equations (all from **C1**) with a ≠ 0 force
  a15 = a35 = a13 and a24 = a26 = a46 = −a13. That is a = λa\* with λ = −a13.
- `no_solution_14`, `_25`, `_36`, `_14_25`, `_14_36`, `_25_36`: when one or two (o,e) chords are also shifted, 6 equations

- `full_support_L_vanishes`: a linear form in the nine X's with the Adler zero on all six legs is identically 0. So

**Lean-proved converse (`ShiftForcedConverse`, L1):**

## 3. Verdict against the pre-registration

- PASS needs (i) the scan to find exactly {λa\*} for C1(4)+C2+C3, and (ii) L1 and L2 to be in Lean with standard axioms.
  (i) holds (table, first row). (ii) holds with the declared split. **PASS.**
- Reported as pre-registered: C2 alone does nothing, and C3 is essential. C1 alone already fixes a on the six-chord
  support. Neither C2 nor C4 (cyclic symmetry) is needed.

## 4. Ablation: which conditions do the work

1. **C1(4) (pion scaling, finite nonzero limit)** does most of the work. It kills 503 supports outright and **by itself
   forces a ∝ a\* once the (o,e) chords are unshifted** (`forced_main` uses only C1 equations). The relative sign
   between (e,e) and (o,o) and the equal magnitudes both come from C1.
2. **C3 (Adler zero)** is needed only to rule out also shifting X14, X25, X36. Under C1 alone those supports carry
   families of dimension 1 to 7. Three consecutive legs suffice, and no two legs do.
3. **C2 (only even-point poles)** is redundant. Every survivor of C1 already shifts all six forbidden chords, so no
   forbidden pole can remain.
4. **Nonzero clause of C1** is needed only to kill the full-support family that satisfies everything else.

## 6. What remains assumed

- **The ansatz.** Linear, δ-uniform shifts of the nine planar X's. Adjacent s_{i,i+1} are unshifted. There are no
  non-linear shifts, no chord-dependent powers of δ, and no deformations other than shifts. This is the main remaining
  assumption.
- **The conditions.** C1 at k = 4 is pion power counting (f_π² = δ). The Adler zero is the defining property of
  Goldstone bosons, so it is put in, not derived.
- n = 6 only. n = 8 is open.
- The step from the conditions to the equations is script-verified, not Lean-verified.

## 7. What this means for "pions from geometry"

Before M2e, the chain was: associahedron canonical form + **a chosen shift** → pions. Now, within linear shifts,
the shift is not a choice. "Finite at pion scaling" fixes it once the (o,e) chords are left alone, and the Adler zero
on three consecutive legs forbids shifting them. So the input rule becomes two physical requirements: pion power
counting and soft-pion behaviour. This is fewer assumptions, but not zero. Masses and GeV are still absent.

## 8. Kill-criteria check

There was no fitting, and the target and conditions were fixed before computing. Nothing rests on "all is one". The
pre-registered claim could have failed: the C1-only and two-leg families show that the scan could find extra
solutions, and did find them where the conditions were weaker. No kill criterion is triggered.
