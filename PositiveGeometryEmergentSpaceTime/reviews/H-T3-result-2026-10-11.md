## (c) Carrier + modulation: PARTIAL

Scripts: `scripts/ht3_abjm_measure.py` and `scripts/ht3_star_triangle.py`, with outputs.

**Method** [SIMULATED, 40 digits]:
- Pull Lee's measure d^{k×2k}C δ(CηCᵀ)/(vol GL(k) ΠM) back through GP's map to the couplings, using the coarea
  formula.
- This is independent of HWX's Jacobian rules.
- Divide by the carrier ω_I = Π dlog sinh 2J_e, which was fixed in the prereg before computing.

**Result in the G2 convention** (odd columns = I, HW's cyclic gauge), j = 1 and j = 2 alike:

| Cell | R = Ω_ABJM / ω_I |
|---|---|
| k = 2, one edge | **1/4, constant** (4 random points, to 10⁻²⁰) |
| k = 3, Y network (L₁, L₂, L₃) | **(1/8)(1 + sech 2L₁ sech 2L₂ sech 2L₃)**, exact to all printed digits at every sample |
| k = 3, Δ network (K₁₂, K₁₃, K₂₃) | **(1/8)(1 + tanh 2K₁₂ tanh 2K₁₃ tanh 2K₂₃)**, likewise |

**G1 convention** (first k columns = I): R is non-constant even at k = 2. This is HW's dθ/s, which is not a dlog form.
Per Amendment A1, the verdict uses G2 uniformly in k.

**Star–triangle.** Solving Y ↦ Δ by matching boundary correlations:
det(∂ log sinh 2K / ∂ log sinh 2L) = −J_Y/J_Δ at four random points (15 digits).
- So the carrier alone is **not** invariant: unlike H-T2's electrical dlog, the determinant is not ±1.
- The product carrier × J **is** invariant, up to sign. That is consistent: the ABJM measure is intrinsic to the point
  of OG≥0, and both networks give the same point.

**What this means:**
- **4-pt.** The Ising carrier *is* the ABJM measure. So the push-forward through the kinematic δ-functions reproduces
  HW's A₄ integrand identically. The superamplitude normalisation δ³(P)δ⁶(Q)/(⟨12⟩⟨23⟩) is CITED from HW, not
  re-derived.
- **6-pt.** The amplitude measure is carrier × J. J = 1 + (product over the single closed medial loop) has the shape
  HWX describe: a product of c's or s's according to orientation [CITED for the rule; SIMULATED for our Ising
  version].
- **KW duality.** In Ising language, KW duality maps the Y network to the Δ network and swaps s ↔ c (GP: s_{e*} = c_e).
  It therefore exchanges J_Y = 1 + Πs and J_Δ = 1 + Πc, as it should [SIMULATED; consistency check].
- **Answer to GP's §9 question.** GP asked to "understand [HWX's] Jacobian in the language of planar Ising networks".
  For n = 3 the answer is: J = 1 + Π_{star edges} sech 2L, or equivalently 1 + Π_{triangle edges} tanh 2K. **Correction (2026-10-12, H-T3b):** this is HWX's
  single-loop J and their eq. (2.55), translated through GP's dictionary. It is **not new** as mathematics; only the
  Ising-variable form and the independent derivation are ours. See `reviews/H-T3b-result-2026-10-12.md`.

**Why PARTIAL, not PASS.** Per the pre-registered rule, R is constant at k = 2 but at k = 3 equals an HWX-type J.

**Plain-words reading of "carrier + modulation".**
- The invariant thing is not the bare network dlog measure. It is that measure times a loop correction J.
- J is built from the same edge variables, so the carrier needs a "self-interaction" term once the network has a closed
  face.
- After that, the modulation (kinematic δ-functions) does produce the ABJM amplitude. That part is by construction:
  the integrand is Lee's.
- So the analogy holds with an amendment: *carrier × loop factor + modulation = amplitude*.

**Caveats:**
- Square roots and branches. The OG two-component issue enters only through the gauge convention and sign. The 6-pt
  localisation (two solutions with √) was not evaluated, since only measures were compared.
- 4 sample points per case. The fits are exact to 12+ digits, but they are not symbolic proofs.
- The G2 convention is an input (Amendment A1).

## (d) Side test, E₄ vs A_{8,3,2}: FAIL

Script: `scripts/ht3_side_e4_amplituhedron.py`, output in `scripts/ht3_side_output.txt`.

**Findings:**
- **Inside A.** Generic E₄ points (H-T2 top network, 6 conductances) map into A_{8,3,2}: ⟨Y i i+1⟩ > 0 for
  i = 1…7, and ⟨Y 1 j⟩ has 3 sign flips [SIMULATED, exact].
- **Not full-dimensional.** The Jacobian rank of Y (gauge-fixed, in Gr(3,5)) w.r.t. the 6 conductances is **5**:
  - smallest/largest singular value ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ to 10⁻¹¹, against ≈ 10⁻² for the Plücker map itself;
  - the same for the moment curve, a random-node moment curve and a random Gaussian Z.
- **Facets.** The 4 facets of E₄ are deletions of (1,2), (2,3), (2,c) and contraction of (4,c). None lands on any
  ⟨Y i i+1⟩ = 0, so none is on ∂A.

**Structural reason:**
- All E₄ row spaces lie in one fixed 6-dim subspace W ⊂ R⁸ [SIMULATED]. W is orthogonal to the two alternating
  vectors.
- There is a unique skew form on W making them isotropic [SIMULATED]. So E₄ is open in LG(3, W), which has dim 6.
  This is CITED: Bychkov–Gorbounov–Kazakov–Talalaev 2109.13952, E_n ⊂ LG(n−1, V).
- For any Z, Y = C·Z restricted to W is a projection from a line ℓ. At a Lagrangian point the tangent space is Sym²C*,
  and the fibre direction is C* ⊗ α. These always meet in α ⊗ α, so the rank is ≤ 5.
  [Linear-algebra sketch; CONJECTURE-level rigour, consistent with the numerics.]

**Meaning.**
- The carrier note's "dimension match 6 = km" was naive. The electrical space is a Lagrangian (type C) object, and it
  can't tile the type-A amplituhedron.
- The Ising analogue is the type-D OG. The ABJM *orthogonal momentum amplituhedron* (2111.03037) is the right
  comparison target, not A_{n,k,2}.
- At n = 2 the image was already known to be a proper sub-segment with no shared boundary.
