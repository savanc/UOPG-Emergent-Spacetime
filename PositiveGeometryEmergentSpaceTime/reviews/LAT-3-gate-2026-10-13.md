## Lesson [ASSESS]
- Current lattice spectra do not constrain subthreshold zeros. Our zeros (MR-2, MR-4) sit exactly where lattice information is weakest.
- Every existing parametrisation either imposes them (circular) or leaves them unconstrained.
- So zero-based lattice tests are structurally blocked until lattice groups add subthreshold information (e.g. dispersive/Roy-constrained lattice analyses) or publish free-zero fits. This is covered as a time-stamped bet by TS1-6/TS1-7/TS1-8.
