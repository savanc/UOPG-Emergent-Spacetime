# Frontier note: POS-GAP, positive geometry for a gapped or confining theory (2026-10-29)

*Written 2026-10-29 by Grok. Notes only. No prereg, no rung, no run. Approved by author approved (2026-10-29).*

**Tags:** CONJECTURE. **Status:** PLANNED, NOT RUN.

## Question

Is there a positive-geometry object (polytope, positroid-type cell, canonical form) for a gapped or confining theory whose
defining data force a mass gap, rather than taking the gap as an input?

## Why now

The KITP shepherding committee's statement (CITED; see notes/TRADE-SPACE-ADDENDUM-YM-EXISTENCE-2026-10-29.md §7.1) says that
even with existence of quantum Yang–Mills claimed, "confinement and the mass gap remain very important open challenges".
Our own record points the same way: CASCADE-1b r2 found the mass ratios free at n = 4, 5, 6 (RATIOS-FREE), and one of
Paper II's hub conjectures is that our objects are blind to quantum numbers.

## Success criterion (mission)

POS-GAP counts as a mission hit **only if** the structure predicts a nonzero gap (or a fixed dimensionless ratio involving it)
with **no extra input**: no mass, scale or ratio put in by hand or fitted. A geometry that merely accommodates a gap is
recorded as FM-FREE, not a hit.

## Out of scope

The Clay Millennium problem (existence and mass gap for 4D Yang–Mills) is explicitly out of scope: it fails the
feasibility gate. POS-GAP asks a structural question about our objects, not for a constructive QFT proof.
