## Corrections to the rollout note itself (errata)
1. MR-1b convention. The rollout (and v7 `verdicts.py`, `defs_glossary.py`) gave the coefficient-to-coupling map as
   g² ∝ c_J/(2J+1), normalised to g_ρ² = 1/2. That is wrong. The prereg (`prereg/MR-1b-ls-rho3-f4-2026-10-12.md`, "AHRV 2.12
   normalisation") and the `LSResidue` docstring use c_J = −g² m² (AHRV eq. 2.12). The papers use the correct convention;
   `verdicts.py`, `defs_glossary.py`, `blueprint.tsv` and `GLOSSARY.md` are corrected.
2. "fusion-rule (WZW) matrix" was a misexpansion. FR-WZW matrices are linear constraints on local parity-odd, WZW-type
   numerators ε(p1,p2,p3,p4)·P(X) of the five-point pion amplitude (deg P ≤ k, reflection sign ±), not WZW fusion rules.
   Corrected in the papers and the alignment files.
3. JORD-1: g_SM lives in Der J3(O), not "Der h3(O)". Exactness window: k | N·c with c the per-generation SU(2)-instanton-vertex
   charge; linear anomaly window = Ibáñez–Ross SU(2)²–Z_k condition. Hankel/Toeplitz: H_ij = C(t_i+t_j); unreflected minor
   C(a)C(b) − C(0)C(a+b). Corrected in `defs_glossary.py` and `GLOSSARY.md`.
`ROLLOUT-BLUEPRINT-2026-11-03.md` and `BLUEPRINT-ALL.md` are left as records of the v7 audit; this note supersedes them on these points.
