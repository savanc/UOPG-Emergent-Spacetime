## Paper claims that are ambiguous or overstated (proposed wording; papers NOT edited)

1. **MR-3, Paper II fig:mr3 caption.** "Only the J=0 coefficient c0 … can fail positivity" holds only on the physical range. The blind proof shows that c1 < 0 for r > 1/2 (PROVED, `orig_c1_neg_above_half`).
   - Proposed: "*On 0<r<1/2* only the J=0 coefficient … can fail positivity; c0 vanishes at r=1/2, and c1<0 for r>1/2."
2. **BR-4, Paper II Result.** "cannot fix any quantity that is homogeneous of non-zero degree". The original Lean has `k : ℕ`, assumes invariance under all c ≠ 0, and needs one c with c^k ≠ 1. Negative degrees (for example a length scale ∝ m⁻¹) are not covered by the original, though the blind proves them (PROVED, `blind_negative_degree`).
   - Proposed: "… homogeneous of non-zero (natural) degree under the scaling group; negative degrees follow by the same argument (blind check)." Alternatively, generalise the original to `k : ℤ`.
   - The paper also does not state what "scale-blind" quantifies over (all nonzero scalars versus positive ones). Suggest saying "invariant under all non-zero rescalings".
3. **LEAN-CERT-1, Paper II.** "N_ACC=2 (the number of hypercharge solutions)" is ambiguous until a normalisation is fixed. With Y_Q free there is a line of solutions (blind `ambiguity witness`). Projectively, or modulo u↔d, the count changes.
   - Proposed: "N_ACC=2: with Y_Q normalised (Y_Q=1), exactly two rational solutions, exchanged by u↔d."
   - "ABHY count for n=5,6,7": the blind proves dim ker = n(n−3)/2 for all n ≥ 3. The paper could say "for all n≥3 (blind second formalisation), with explicit planar bases certified at n=5,6,7".
4. **M3, Paper I line ~562.** "the band edge is 3.84σ away … checked in Lean". The original Lean (`be14_gap`) proves only > 3.8σ, so the 3.84 figure was not itself Lean-checked.
   - Both readings are now PROVED to round to 3.84: the raw BE14 propagation (`orig_nsigma_rounds`) and the rounded −3.79 ± 0.31 (`M3Blind.nsigma_rounds`).
   - Proposed: keep "3.84σ" and cite the compare theorem. Alternatively, say "more than 3.8σ (3.84σ)".
5. **MR-1b.** The sign convention c_J = −g²m² flips the literal residue (PROVED: original = −blind for ρ3 and f4). The v8.1 glossary already says that quoted ratios are ratios of magnitudes, so **no further change is needed**. This rung is recorded as the clearest case where a convention transcribed by hand is invisible to Lean.
6. **CAPPED-FORMAN-1 / HG-DIM-2.**
   - The papers define g_k(m) (glossary, curvature §Lean), so it is not undefined. The blind formalisation omitted the g_k optimisation, which is a blind-side gap, not a paper issue.
   - Minor point: the papers' "for all k" in "max g_k = 1 − min(3,k−3)" implicitly assumes k ≥ 3. The original uses ℕ-subtraction, so `small_face_gap` shows a different value for k < 3. Proposed: "for all k≥3".
   - The Kendall-τ = Cayley-distance step (HG-DIM-2) and the Birkhoff/assignment step (BFS assignment minima) are CITED in the blind. The originals avoid them with walk and dual certificates, so no paper change is needed.
7. **GRADED-POSITROID.** The weight normalisation of the generating function is a convention. Both sides use the same ACC text, so the verdict is EQUIV, with no change needed.
