# Alignment Phase B: sanity theorems, 2026-11-03

Phase B adds sanity and tie theorems for the rungs whose Lean definitions were NAME-ONLY (the definition is
correct only by its name or docstring) or whose wording was checked by hand in Phase A. Each module checks
(a) known values, (b) that a planted wrong case is rejected and (c) that the hypotheses can be met (non-vacuity).
Where it was feasible, a module also proves that the definition *is* the object the paper names (a tie).

## Paper errors found (papers NOT edited)

1. **MR-1b sign wording (Paper II, around main.tex 296–302, the MR-1b card; abstract row 7).** Applying the
   stated convention c_J = −g²m² literally gives g_ρ² = −1/2, g_ρ₃² = +5/32 and g_f₄² = +49/480. The signed
   ratios are therefore −5/16 and −49/240. The quoted 5/16 and 49/240 are ratios of magnitudes |c_J|/m². The
   numbers are unchanged; only the wording is wrong.
   * Proposed correction: write "g_J² = |c_J|/m² (c_J = −g²m² up to the sign of the residue normalisation)".
     Alternatively, state that the ratios are of |g_J²|.

No other sanity theorem contradicted a paper claim.
