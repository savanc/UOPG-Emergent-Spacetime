# Alignment pilot: prose vs Lean (2026-10-31)

Question (Paper II p. 9 calls prose-to-Lean alignment "checked by hand" and "the weaker link"):
can alignment be checked mechanically, at what cost, and which method pays?

Three rungs, three methods each:

1. **Blueprint row**: prose claim, Lean name, full statement, dependencies, literal reading, gaps.
2. **Sanity theorems**: a known value, a planted wrong case that is rejected, and a non-vacuity check.
3. **Blind second formalisation**: statements written from the prose *before* reading the original
   Lean (commit `c79457b`), then compared by proof (equivalence or one direction) or by a precise mismatch.

Tags: PROVED = `lake build` passed, no `sorry`, standard axioms only. SIMULATED = interpreter or
Python run (run id given). CITED = fetched this session. CONJECTURE / SPECULATION as usual.

### Blind formalisation

`Surf1Blind`: my own oriented-ribbon/ghost colour-loop enumerator `coeffs` (W = Σ_orient Σ_ghost (−c)^k N^(loops−k)),
graphs built from the prose description (theta, K₄, double square `dsq`, K₃,₃ for the crossed box),
`ClaimClosedForm` and `ClaimEnumerated` (K_tri(3) = −1 ∧ ∀N≥4 K_box^× < 0 ∧ ∀N≥3 disjunction).

### Comparison (PROVED unless tagged)

- `blind_theta`, `blind_K4`, `blind_dsq`: the blind enumerator, evaluated in the kernel, gives exactly the
  original's Laurent data. `evalW_theta/K4/dsq`: `evalW = ev thetaL/tetraL/ttUL` for N ≠ 0. So the blind
  graphs K₄ and dsq are the original's tetra and ttU.
- `Ktri_eq`: blind K_tri = original `K (W1 tetra) (W1 theta)` for N ≥ 3. `blind_Ktri_three`, `blind_closed_form : ClaimClosedForm`.
- K₃,₃: the blind coefficient vector [N^-3: 1280, N^-1: −1728, N: 480, N³: −32] equals the original `boxUL`.
  This is **SIMULATED** (run ALIGN-PILOT-K33-EVAL-20261031, Lean interpreter `#eval … == K33vec` → `true`, 33 s,).
  Kernel `decide` runs out of memory above 12 GB even on one of 64 orientation chunks (512 ghost masks × 36 slots).
  `claimEnumerated_of_K33 : coeffs K33 = K33vec → ClaimEnumerated` is PROVED, so the full blind claim is
  conditional only on that one vector. The prototype colour enumerator in Python (`<local path>`,
  run ALIGN-PILOT-COLOUR-PY-20261031, SIMULATED) gives the same four vectors. This independently supports the
  docstring identification boxU ≅ K₃,₃ and ttU ≅ double square. The same run shows the identification matters:
  the other cubic 6-vertex graph (the prism) gives K = (N²−12)²/(2N(N²−4)) ≥ 0, so the sign claim depends on the
  crossed box being K₃,₃. That identification is a docstring in the original, not a theorem.

### Sanity (PROVED)

- Known value: `Ktri_values` (K_tri(3) = −1, K_tri(4) = 1); `single_loop_is_dim` (one loop gives N² − 1).
- Planted wrong cases rejected: `planted_tri_alone_false` and `planted_box_alone_false`. Neither disjunct
  alone holds for all N ≥ 3, so both are needed. `box_pos_at_3` (K_box(3) = 8/15 > 0), `tri_pos_ge_4`.
- Non-vacuity: the hypotheses are numeric ranges (N ≥ 3), so they are trivially inhabited.

### Finding

Aligned. The blind design ended up structurally close to the original because both follow prereg §2.
The real added value is the independent recomputation of the graph data, which caught nothing wrong.

### Blueprint row

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Prose (Paper II ~l. 481) | "The per-edge formula and the mean … are proved on paper for all n≥4 PAPERPROVED, with the general algebra and kernel-checked instances n=5,…,9 in Lean PROVED; … The mean changes sign at the decagon"; "Negative edges exist exactly for n≥7 … minimum −1/5 at n=7" |
| Lean | `HgDim1Mean.sumP_closed (m)`, `mean_kappa_closed (m)`, `sumP_instances`, `enum_eq_sumP` (n = 5..8), `enum_eq_sumP_9`, `mean_kappa_instance`, `tris_complete_5/6/7`, `check_5..9`, `nodup_8/9`, `counts_5..9`; `HgDim1.edge_neg` etc. (two K₇ edges) |
| Literal reading | (a) Closed-form algebra: (2 − sumP m / ((m+1)C_{m+2}))/(m+2) = (6−m)/((m+2)(2m+3)) for all m. (b) An enumerated list of diagonal sets has the right Σp for n = 5..9. (c) `mean_kappa_instance` is five rational-number identities. |
| Gaps | **(G1)** `mean_kappa_instance` is pure numerals and is not linked to the enumeration or to κ. **(G2)** No κ (Ollivier curvature) or flip-graph object appears in the mean theorems. The per-edge formula κ = (2−p)/(n−2) is in Lean only for two K₇ edges (`HgDim1.lean`). So "kernel-checked instances n=5..9" is accurate for the Σp counting identity only; the mean *of Ollivier curvature* is conditional on the paper proof of the per-edge formula. **(G3)** Completeness of the enumeration at n = 8, 9 rests on validity + nodup + count = Catalan (cited), not on a comparison with a subset enumeration (done only for 5..7). **(G4)** The sign change at the decagon is not stated in Lean. |

### Blind formalisation

`HgDim1Blind`: my own recursive triangulation enumerator (apex of side (i,j)), my own `pval`, the blind
`sumP`, `meanKappaLocal`, `ClaimSumP`, `ClaimMeanLocal`, and `ClaimMeanConditional` (for any κ on (T,d) pairs obeying
the per-edge formula, the mean equals (10−n)/((n−2)(2n−5)) for n = 5..9). The blind statement made gap G2
explicit by design, because it had to state the per-edge formula as a hypothesis.

### Comparison (PROVED)

- `same_set_n` (n = 5..9): the blind triangulation list is a permutation of the original's (via the original `key`).
  `same_p_n`: blind `pval` = original `pOf` on every (T,d). Two independent enumerators agree at n = 8, 9,
  which partly addresses G3 (independent agreement, though still not a completeness proof).
- `claim_sumP`, `sumP_matches_original` (blind Σp = original `sumP (n−4)`), and `mean_targets_agree`
  ((10−n)/((n−2)(2n−5)) = (6−m)/((m+2)(2m+3)) at n = m+4, for all m).
- `claim_mean_conditional : ClaimMeanConditional` and `claim_mean_local : ClaimMeanLocal`, through the general lemmas
  `sum_inner`, `sum_outer`, `mean_of_formula`. This proves the link G1 lacks: the mean over the actual enumeration
  of (2−p)/(n−2) is the closed form, for n = 5..9.

### Sanity (PROVED)

- Known value: `pentagon_p_one` (every p = 1 on the pentagon, so κ = 1/3 on the 5-cycle).
- Prose check: `no_negative_6` (p ≤ 2 on K₆) and `negative_7` (some p = 3 on K₇, so κ = −1/5), matching

- Planted wrong case: `planted_wrong_mean` (2n−6 in place of 2n−5 is rejected at n = 5).
- Sign change: `sign_change` (closed form > 0 ⇔ m < 6, = 0 ⇔ m = 6, i.e. n = 10). This fills G4.
- Non-vacuity: `claim_mean_local` instantiates the conditional claim with κ := the local formula.

### Finding

Real wording gap (G1/G2). Suggested prose: "the counting identity Σp = … and the mean of the local formula
(2−p)/(n−2) over the enumeration are kernel-checked for n=5,…,9 PROVED; that this local formula is the Ollivier
curvature is proved on paper for all n and in Lean for two K₇ edges." (Suggestion only; Paper II not edited.)

## Rung 3: RG-IR-1 (affine structure of the Yukawa/gauge flow)

### Blueprint row

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Prose (Paper II ~l. 471) | "Lean proves the affine structure of the flow in seven theorems … this certifies the algebra, not a physics claim." |
| Lean | `RgIr1.u_flow_affine (y G κ a) (hy : y≠0) : (−2/y³)(yκ(ay²−G)) = 2κ(G/y²−a)`; `qcd_ratio_fixed`; `qcd_sensitivity_exponent : −8/(−7) − 1 = 1/7 ∧ 0 < 1/7`; `exp_ratio_invariant`; `affine_width (S A u0 u1)`; `affine_contracts`; `sm_exponent_signs` |

| Gaps | **(R1)** No derivative or ODE appears; `u_flow_affine` is a field identity, the chain rule is implicit. **(R2)** The UV→IR map u_IR = S·u_UV + A is *assumed* by `affine_width`/`affine_contracts`, not derived from the flow. So "proves the affine structure of the flow" overstates; "certifies the algebra" (same sentence) is accurate. **(R3)** The second conjunct of `qcd_sensitivity_exponent` is the detached literal `0 < 1/7` and is not about the exponent expression. |

### Blind formalisation

`RgIr1Blind`: `ClaimLinearises` (HasDerivAt form: y′ = y(ay²−G)/k ⇒ (1/y²)′ = (2G/y² − 2a)/k), `ClaimAffine`
(the difference of two solutions solves the homogeneous flow), `ClaimWidth`, `ClaimQcdExponent`.

### Comparison (PROVED)

- `claim_linearises`, `linearises_via_original` (the original `u_flow_affine` is exactly the derivative value; fills R1).
- `claim_affine`; `affine_time_map` (u₁(t) − u₂(t) = e^{A(t)−A(0)}(u₁(0) − u₂(0)) for u′ = αu + β, given an antiderivative A of α);
  `affine_UV_IR` (∃ S > 0, B, every solution has u(t) = S·u(0) + B). This fills R2 for a generic linear flow.
- `width_iff`: the blind width claim ⇐ the original (the blind version has superfluous hypotheses 0 < A, lo ≤ hi; the original is stronger).
- `qcd_iff`: the blind claim ⇔ the original. The blind one states the positivity about the expression, which is how R3 should read.

### Sanity (PROVED)

- Known value: `known_fixed_ratio` (R = 2/9).
- Planted wrong cases: `planted_inverse_not_affine` (u = 1/y does not linearise) and `planted_wrong_b3` (b₃ = +7 gives a negative exponent).
- Non-vacuity: `nonvacuous_linearises` (y ≡ 1), `nonvacuous_contracts` (S = 0.0768 from the RG-IR-1 v2 run, SIMULATED value), `nonvacuous_affine_UV_IR`.

### Finding

Real wording gap (R2). It is now closed in Lean by `affine_UV_IR` (generic linear flow; the SM coefficient
functions are not formalised). Suggested prose: "Lean proves that 1/y² obeys a linear flow and that any linear

## Recommendation for scaling (~77 rungs)

1. **Blueprint rows for every rung (do first).** About 15–25 min per rung, so ~20–30 h in total. They found
   all three real mismatches (HG G1/G2, RG R2, RG R3) by literal reading alone. Best value per cost.
2. **Sanity theorems (known value, planted wrong, non-vacuity) for every rung.** About 20–40 min per rung,
   ~25–50 h. Cheap, and they catch vacuous or detached statements (R3-type) and missing claims (HG G4).
3. **Blind second formalisation only for headline rungs** (those in an abstract or conclusion), roughly 10–15 rungs at
   1–3 h each, ~15–40 h. Costliest, and only partly blind. Its distinct value here was (a) forcing G2 into the open
   by having to state the hypothesis, (b) independent recomputation of graph data (SURF), and (c) filling R2.
   A cheaper variant is "state the prose claim in Mathlib-only terms and prove it from the original", the
   Challenge/Solution pattern below.

Prior art (CITED, fetched 31 Oct 2026): the PFR project page (https://teorth.github.io/pfr/) describes a
"Palomar" Challenge/Solution pair: `PFRPalomar/Challenge.lean` states headline theorems "in Lean-core-and-Mathlib
terms only", and `Solution.lean` proves them from the project, with a `formalization.yaml` recording "the
correspondence with the papers, and the limitations". Our `Blind/*.lean` files (Mathlib-only imports) plus the `*Sanity.lean`
comparison theorems are the same shape. leanblueprint (https://github.com/PatrickMassot/leanblueprint, fetched;
"plasTeX plugin to build formalization blueprints") is the standard tool for the blueprint-row layer.
I did not read its documentation further, so I make no claim about its features.

## Honest limits

- **Partial blindness.** The blind statements were written by an agent that had seen the paper prose, the prereg,
  and earlier session context about these rungs, though not the original Lean files. Shared conventions
  (prereg §2 colour model) made the SURF design converge with the original. This is a second formalisation by the
  same team, not an independent one.
- K₃,₃ colour data is SIMULATED (interpreter + Python), not kernel-checked; the full blind SURF claim is conditional on it.
- HG: enumerator agreement at n = 8, 9 is between two programs, not a completeness proof. The per-edge Ollivier
  formula is still not formalised in general (the conditional form only makes that explicit).
- RG: `affine_UV_IR` is for a generic linear flow. The SM-specific coefficient functions and the run's S value are not in Lean.
- The new modules are not in `import PositiveGeometryEmergentSpaceTime`. They are audited by `lean/AlignmentAxioms.lean`, not by AxiomsAll.
- Paper II wording suggestions above are proposals only; no paper or locked file was edited.
